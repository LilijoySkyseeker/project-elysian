## 1. MORPHOLOGY, SCALE & EXTERNAL FORM
- **Body Plan:** Hexapod taurform — two humanoid arms, four prehensile lower paws, prehensile tail. One pectoral girdle and two pelvic girdles (DOC-01A §2). **No torso segment:** the arms hang from a short muscular *yoke* at the front of a horizontal thorax, almost on top of the fore limbs; "upright" is a reared posture (DOC-01A §2B).
- **Dimensions:** Trunk (chest front → tail root) 1.00–1.15 m (reference 1.10 m); chest front → tail tip 1.90–2.05 m; mass 50–70 kg (reference individual 60 kg). Standing on all six: ~45 cm at the withers, yoke crest and arm shoulder ~0.50 m, top of head ~0.70 m; reared (sit-tall): shoulder ~0.80 m, top of head ~1.15 m, ear tips ~1.28 m. Low-slung feline/mustelid/vulpine spine — one tube: a long deep chest (0.45 m, 0.21 × 0.23 m, ~69 cm girth) running back into a slimmer flexible loin (0.65 m, 0.15 × 0.16 m, ~49 cm girth), chest-to-loin girth ≈ 1.4 — a fox's outline on a corgi's legs, drawn out to a marten's length; 180° head rotation.
- **Limbs & Manipulators:**
  - **2 Upper Arms:** Dexterous humanoid hands for fine tool work; ~340 N grip; not rated as anchors.
  - **4 Lower Paws:** **Hand-paws, not feet** (DOC-01A §3B): five digits with a fully opposable first digit (flat nail, no claw) and a semi-divergent fifth, closing on a rail like a three-jaw chuck; ~10–11 cm long, short and thick. Tactile pads carry the conductive grounding film and do all routine gripping (~280 N each, ~450 N momentary); semi-retractable claws on D2–D5 kept sheathed on habitat surfaces. Furred on the back, bare and glossy on the gripping face; near-full wrist supination. Working posture (hind paws + tail) holds 1.7 g; storm posture (all anchors) 2.7 g.
  - **Prehensile Tail (0.88 m, ~7 kg — 12 % of body mass):** A sixth limb, not an ornament: base ~17 cm through (21 cm furred), tapering over its length. Fifth anchor (~450 N); the counterweight that brings the front-heavy chassis from ~67/33 to a ~56/44 load split and lets the front end rear without the hind paws unloading; the rotational reaction mass that makes the half-second vector-flip possible (DOC-01A §3A); houses the VHF antenna at λ/4 (D-1).
- **Coat & Phenotype:** Dense plush fur with melanin-metal micro-complexes (mildly conductive; radiation shielding); pale underfur lifted by an albedo reflex in direct sun; enormous swivelling ears (radiators, RF arrays, acoustic pinnae); large eyes. Cute on purpose.
- **Builds:** Reversible void-build (lighter skeleton, grip endurance) and ground-build (denser, sprint and carry) phenotypes, retuned by the Core over weeks to months (DOC-01A §5).

## 2. THORACIC CORE & HEAD CAVITY ENGINEERING
- **Central Nervous System Relocation:** The Primary Cortical Brain and the Genomic Bio-Core sit in the upper thoracic Neuro-Thoracic Vault inside an Endo-Thoracic Box, behind an interlocking double rib cage, in non-Newtonian CSF (DOC-01B §1).
- **Cranial Turret:** No primary brain. Houses edge-processing sensory nodes and RF pre-amps, the food-preprocessing engine (high-torque jaws, pharyngeal grinding plates, potent salivary glands), acoustic resonating chambers, the ear coolant loops, and hydrated gel shock matrices.
- **Genomic Bio-Core (DOC-01C):** The complement of the brain — exact, lossless, structured. Three substrates: neural (RF front-end, RADAR echo processing), biomineral crystal (exact arithmetic, the archive index, the autonomic flight controller), nucleic acid (archive memory, genome compile, identity keys). ~600 g, 2/8/20 W. Experienced as a perfect assistant — an inner voice with no will. Brain and Core are interdependent; loss of either is brain death.
- **Biological Non-Senescence:** No aging; telomere degradation eliminated; somatic surveillance by the Core; a whitelist immune system that clears anything failing the self-checksum (no infectious disease, no transplants, a licensed microbiome). Death comes only from trauma or catastrophe; median life ~280 years, some past a thousand (DOC-02B §8).
- **Regeneration:** Everything except brain and Core regrows, at developmental pace — a digit in a year, a limb in six, an eye in three with vision returning slowly after (DOC-01A §2A).
- **Vesica oxygenii:** A pressurised O₂ bladder (~1.8 L at ~400 kPa, ~7.2 L STP) charged by a gas gland and rete mirabile, in the Cardio-Respiratory Vault; carries the vacuum oxygen reserve.

## 4. METABOLISM & METABOLIC CHARGING
- **Diet:** Omnivorous; human rations, high-density paste gels, and copper/zinc/sodium supplements for the bio-RF tissues — metals taste good to them.
- **Energy Profile:** ~2,500 kcal/day baseline (~121 W); 1,600 idle; 5,200–5,700 at sustained peak (DOC-01B §7). Standing costs: the void tax (~175 kcal/day holding bone and muscle in micro-G), re-optimisation surges (~290 kcal/day for weeks) when the Core retunes the body to a new task or gravity, and years of the same during regeneration.
- **Thermal:** Ears (40–46 W in cabin; 6–80 W throttle in vacuum), respiratory mucosa, ventral thermal windows, paw and tail sinks (DOC-01B §8); the pelt dominates vacuum heat loss (DOC-01E.2 §3).
