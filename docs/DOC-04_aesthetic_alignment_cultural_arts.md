## 1. DESIGNER QUIRK: NEURO-COGNITIVE AESTHETIC HARDWIRING
- **Creator Intent:** The foundation's half of the specification (DOC-10 §2): direct neural pathways linking the perception of human-spectrum proportions, colour harmonies and musical scales to limbic dopamine/oxytocin release. It is why the eyes are human-grade colour with a fourth cone (DOC-01F §2) and the ears hear the human band at full musical fidelity (DOC-01F §5): the quirk needs the senses it runs on.
- **Cultural Value:** Aethela experience genuine frisson from human visual art, music, literature and cinema. Kin hear more overtones than humans and favour rich timbres; they see finer hue steps and favour subtle colour.

## 2. KIN VARIATIONS OF HUMAN MEDIUMS
- **Dual-Spectrum Canvases:** Pigment for humans, plus raised dielectric/metallic strokes — centimetres proud of the surface, since bio-RADAR resolves ~2 cm — that return a pulse as a second, spatial composition only a Kin perceives.
- **Polyphonic Whistle-Choral:** Low human-like vocal resonance from the larynx with two independent whistle-lines from the dual whistle organ (DOC-01F §5C), to ~30 kHz, layered over human harmonic scales. Above 20 kHz the choral has parts no human audience hears, and the Kin do not always say so.
- **Hexapod Zero-G Choreography:** 3-D rotational ballet on all six limbs and the tail — vector-flips, push-glides, tail-catches (DOC-01A §6) — structured explicitly for human visual appreciation.
- **Retelling:** The Kin's own oldest art form, and the one humans least notice: carried memory told at a Weave, in Kin-code, third-person, formulaic on the arks and sprawling in Sol (DOC-01C §8). Nothing of it is written down.
- **The bands humans cannot reach, and the argument** (DOC-13 §3, §6; D-84): composed Shares, Burst poetry, tetrachromat light-work, Hum-singing, station-tuning — and the **Plain School**, the species' standing experiment in what it would love if the quirk had not told it, unresolved on purpose.

## 3. WHAT THE KIN DO NOT MAKE
- **Records.** No Kin literature exists as text; no Kin history as document. The retelling is the form, and its loss is the form's condition (D-19).
- **Medicine, law, archives** — human institutions the Kin use and do not build.
