## 1. KIN-NEST ERGONOMICS (HABITAT SPECIFICATIONS)
- **3D Rail Grid Matrices:** Zero-G corridors are cylindrical, lined with high-friction carbon webbing. Aethela lock on with paw pads (claws stay sheathed — the webbing lasts) and move by rail-walk, brachiation and push-glide in any orientation (DOC-01A §6). Working posture on a rail holds 1.7 g; storm posture 2.7 g.
- **RF-Transparent Bulkheads:** Interior walls of composite polymers transparent to 1.5–10 GHz, so no living space isolates the mesh.
- **Zero-Light, Quiet, Scent-Clean:** Kin sectors run in optical darkness to save energy — and quiet, and scent-clean by habit and filtration, because a station is loud in every sense to a Kin (DOC-01F §1) — and because a clean sector is a *canvas*: the Kin are scent-literate, not scent-averse, and compose on it (DOC-13 §4, D-84). Machine status and environmental cues are broadcast as ambient RF beacons; **alerting for Kin is never a klaxon.** Kin-sector displays run at ≥120 Hz; human displays visibly flicker to them.
- **Silence Chambers:** Padded, scented with the Cluster, and equipped with a 0.01 W beacon so the link layer stays up while the application layer is quiet — a psychological reset without triggering The Silence.
- **Post-Storm Standards (from ~AF 100, DOC-10 §4):** After the Shielded Storm revealed that Kin were not in the shelter plan, every shielded storm shelter must include Kin-sized space and an internal beacon; every EM-opaque room a beacon; every Kin sector a Silence Chamber. These are standards, not original design.
- **Thermal windows:** Cold conductive bulkhead panels are provided in Kin sectors for ventral "splooting" (DOC-01B §8).
- **Burn webs (D-75):** Every Kin sector and every Kin-crewed vessel carries aft-facing web panels — the surface a hard burn presses a Kin *into*, since above 2.7 g grip is moot and the body must lie flat (DOC-01A §6, burn posture). Human sectors carry couches for the same reason. A Kin on a burn web is conscious, sighted and on the mesh at accelerations that put the human crew under; the panels are placed where a fault would need reaching.

## 2. HARDWARE & DIGITAL INTERFACE
- **Dual Interface Protocol:**
  - **Native Bio-Wireless:** Station computers sit as guest nodes in the Cluster frame; telemetry, control and software run over Bursts and Shares (DOC-01C §4). The Kin *read* a station computer's diagnosis in seconds; the computer does the diagnosing (D-12).
  - **Physical Redundancy:** Physical switches, levers and prehensile-paw controls are retained on all safety-critical hardware for mechanical override and human compatibility.
- **Grounding:** All rails and bulkheads are bonded; a badly grounded sector *itches* — static discharge blinds the near-field pit nodes (DOC-01F §3).
- **Documentation:** Station engineering records are human-authored. Kin crews read them and do not write their own; a Kin-filed log is terse, and one line in it will leak affect (DOC-02 §3).

## 3. MIXED SECTORS
- **Furniture** is the wrong shape. A Kin stands ~45 cm at the withers with the head at ~0.70 m, and reared into sit-tall puts the shoulder at ~80 cm and the top of the head at ~1.15 m — so a human counter is above the head on all six and about chin height reared — reachable, better sat up onto — a human chair is useless under a taur body, and stairs are a ramp problem. Chairs, counters and stairs are built for humans and used by Kin in the sit-tall posture or not at all.
- **Personal space** is not a Kin concept; humans in mixed sectors become, within a week, *surroundings* (DOC-01F §4).
- **Medical:** No Kin medical tradition exists. Human surgeons, prosthetists and the microbiome licensing protocol serve both species (DOC-02B §9).
