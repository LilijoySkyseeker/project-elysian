---

Made to hold humans inside the circle of *mine* (D-79), the Kin are exceptional crisis personnel and dangerous defenders of their habitats and human charges — by anatomy, by mesh, and by doctrine written in their own dead. There is no non-aggression in the design and no leash: what looks like pacifism is what a love does when the person in front of you is also inside the circle. Individually a Kin is the most dangerous thing on any station and the gentlest; collectively the Kin cannot make war on the people they love, and never have.

## 1. CLUSTER MACTAC (MESH-ACTION TACTICAL ARRAY)
In high-threat scenarios — hull breaches, boarding, industrial failure — a Cluster shifts into **MACTAC**:

```
                       [ CLUSTER MACTAC NODE LINK ]
                                     │
           ┌─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┐
           ▼                         ▼                         ▼
   [ UNIFIED VECTOR MAP ]   [ TARGET ASSIGNMENT ]    [ SUB-20 ms EXECUTION ]
   Pooled Echo Cortices     Shared threat priorities  Lattice codec 5–8 ms + neck 2.5 ms
```

- **Sub-20 ms coordination:** Over CLUSTER-tier Shares the Cluster pools Echo Cortex point clouds into one field — if one Kin's RADAR sees around a corner, every member sees it from that angle, ~15 ms later (DOC-01C §2, §4). "Zero-latency" is relative to speech.
- **What the Cores do — and don't:** Lattices handle the exact work — ballistics, decompression rates, timing, the bookkeeping of who is where (D-12). Open-ended tactical judgment stays in the Cortical Brains and the Cluster's shared sense of the room. The Kin are not a distributed supercomputer; they are eight people who cannot be surprised from behind.
- **Cost:** MACTAC is the 20 W Bio-Core peak; a Cluster can hold it for hours in cabin and minutes in vacuum.

## 2. ZERO-LIGHT & VACUUM ENGAGEMENT
- **The Blind Advantage:** In darkness or smoke, Kin operate at full efficiency on bio-RADAR, near-field and hull-hearing (DOC-01F).
- **Vacuum Engagement:** Voiding (DOC-01E §1) leaves a Kin conscious and working for ~17 min at full capacity and ~25 min more at a crawl (DOC-01E.2 §6) — on rails at speed, tools in both hands, ears run as a throttle. Doctrine within the window: **seventeen minutes is the number; the Core will keep saying it.**
- **Hard burn (D-75):** The brain is beside the heart; nothing a ship can do puts a Kin under. Doctrine: *web, tuck, seal* — take an aft-facing burn web (DOC-03 §1), head between the arms so the eyes stay lit, tail along the body, fascia up, breathe from the *vesica*, lock and wait. ~15–20 g for the seconds a hard burn lasts, ~8–10 g for minutes. **During a burn the Kin are the only crew awake:** whatever fails at 8 g, a human in a couch cannot reach it and a Kin can rail-walk to it — six limbs, hands and forepaws on the same rail, tendon-locked at every stop. Kin-crewed tugs and arks burn harder than human-crewed ships for exactly this reason. The humans call the manoeuvre *fox-rated*; the Kin do not call it anything. **And they pay for it afterwards (D-95):** for hours after a hard burn optical vision is grey and comes back slowly, colour last, while RADAR and the near-field are untouched — so the first hour after the burn is worked by a crew who cannot see and do not need to, and the human aboard is the one being led.
- **Solar orientation reflex:** Ear dishes edge-on to the sun, ventral windows to shadow. Bare skin is how the sun gets in; fur is armour (DOC-01E.2 §3C).
- **Storm protocol (post-AF 97):** In a solar event the mesh degrades and the humans are behind EM-opaque walls. Whistle-talk, touch and hull-hearing are the channels; every shelter has a Kin space and a beacon (DOC-03 §1). The dose is inside tolerance and the deaths, when they come, are slow — **Tell before you go.**
- **No juvenile EVA.** Adult vacuum physiology at six — literally: the full seventeen minutes, the organs being on the body clock (D-94) — juvenile judgment, no Reading key. The culture does not send children into the void, and the rule is cultural because the physiology gives it no help (DOC-02B §5).

## 3. CONTAINMENT & DEFENSIVE TACTICS
- **Hull Integrity Priority:** No high-kinetic firearms that risk bulkheads.
- **Preferred Ordnance** — preferred, not required (D-79): the body is the lethal weapon and always was (280 N paws, grinding jaws, a seven-kilo tail, 10 m/s in the dark); a Kin who must can kill, and fewer than ten ever have, every one for someone else (DOC-10 §5). What follows is what a species that reads a violent human as a *frightened* one (DOC-01F §4) reaches for first:
  - **Micro-Microwave Stunners:** Directed energy tuned to disrupt nervous systems without puncturing plating.
  - **Pressurised Sticky-Foam Cannons:** Seal a breach and immobilise an intruder with one shot.
  - **Focused EMP Beams:** Via modified comm-rigs, to disable drones and unshielded electronics.
- **The Silence as a weapon and a wound:** Full EM shielding disables a Kin in thirty minutes (DOC-01C §10). Humans have used it, in disputes, and the Kin have not forgotten which stations. **The one Kin war** (*Dioscuri Yard*, DOC-10 §4, AF ~188): two Clusters with humans on both sides of a dock dispute, both Clusters refusing both sides and holding the channel between them — until one side put the other's Cluster in the Silence to take it off the board — one Kin, Rill-3, in a bonded can with the beacon pulled — and the homing watchdog took her out the outer lock toward the Hum; she missed the far face by eleven metres (S016). No Kin struck another. The Kin count it as their war.
- **Self-defence** is a Cluster function: a Kin alone contains and evades — she is over-matched in the *other* direction — and her mates answer what she will not. The order of the love, when it cannot be contained, is Cluster, then humans, then self (D-79); a Kin will kill a human for a mate, and it is the worst day of her life, and she carries it with the feeling intact.

## 4. RECOVERY DOCTRINE
- **Body recovery.** A fallen Kin's body is recovered for the Reading — warm within hours, frozen within days (DOC-01C §7). Doctrine since the Field (AF 212), where thirty-one were Read on the hull of a tug and nine were heard for two days and never found (DOC-10 §4). *No one left behind* has a price and the Kin pay it. **After the Reading the Cluster burns the body** (D-86, DOC-11 §5) — the keys are in the cells.
- **Denial.** A body that cannot be recovered is burned. An enemy can Read too. A Kin under fire may have to do this to a friend; it is the hardest order in the doctrine and it is given.
- **The Telling before risk.** Before a mission, before transit torpor, before an ark leaves: a year in a week, so the Reading has less to carry.
- **Human management** cuts margins on the belief that Kin tolerate vacuum. They tolerate it for forty-two minutes. The Field is cited at every safety review the Kin are allowed to attend.
