## 1. JOINT HABITAT ARCHITECTURE: *THE SPHERES OF ARTEMIS-9*
Shared Human-Aethela habitats partition space into **Gravity Gradients** for two body plans and two sensory worlds:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    HUMAN SECTOR (1.0G Centripetal)              │
│  Flat floors, stairs, optical lighting, sound-dampened bulkheads │
│  — loud, bright, scented, and the wrong shape                    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                ▲
                                │ Transition Locks & Ramp-Webs
                                ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    KIN SECTOR (Micro-G / 0.0G)                  │
│  Cylindrical corridors, 3D rail matrices, RF-transparent walls, │
│  dark, quiet, scent-clean; ambient RF beacons; Silence Chamber   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **The Transition Locks:** Micro-g junction hubs lined with carbon felt. Humans use handrails; Aethela enter micro-G with a bound and a *tail-catch* on the first rail, and leave it by rail-walking the ramp-web down until the floor is unambiguous (DOC-01A §6). Humans are routed around.
- **Atmospheric & Lighting Modes:** Human sectors lit in warm optical wavelengths, heavily soundproofed. Kin sectors dark or low red, quiet, filtered; status broadcast as ambient RF; alerting never by sound (DOC-03 §1).
- **Post-Storm standards:** Kin-sized shelter space and beacons in every shielded room (DOC-03 §1, DOC-10 §4).
- **Where Kin are:** dense in the spires and the Belt, present on Luna and Mars, thin on Earth; assigned, still mostly by humans, to the hostile postings (DOC-10 §6). Ground-build on the planets, void-build on the rails, and a Weaver never quite tuned for anywhere (DOC-01A §5).

## 2. INTERSTELLAR ARK SHIPS: *THE SILENT VOYAGERS*

### 2A. WHERE THEY CAME FROM (D-108, D-109, D-110)

**Humans built none, and never will.** Not for want of money — for want of a reason. The ceiling is fusion and 3 % of lightspeed, a crossing is two centuries, human lifespan is 120–140 (DOC-12 §2–§3), and **there is no human cold sleep anywhere in this setting**: transit torpor is a Kin capability. *You cannot send a human to another star in this universe.* So an ark was never a privilege granted to the Kin. **It is the one object in five and a half centuries that only the Kin can use — and it is the largest thing anyone has ever built.**

**Labour, not money.** The Kin are the construction workforce of the system: unsuited vacuum, six hands, RADAR in the dark, 2.7 g, no suits, no airlock cycles, no consumables. Every spire and tether in Sol was substantially built by Kin hands. **An ark is not bought. It is built, and the people who build everything can build one.**

**Continuity, which is why it works.** Human megaprojects are relay races — the people who begin them die, and the knowledge crosses the gap in documents, imperfectly. The Kin do not age. **The same Kin who cuts the first spar walks aboard**, and an eighty-year build is one continuous act of attention by one Cluster. So the arks are **the one artefact in the setting where the documentation flaw is not a flaw** (DOC-01C §8, D-101): *archive verbatim and document never* cripples a civilisation and is completely irrelevant on a project where nobody hands over and nobody dies of age. **The Kin are better at this than humans for exactly the reason they are worse at everything else.**

**Where.** The outer Belt and the far postings — the dark side, the relays, the outer moons, where a Kin can go a decade without touching a human (DOC-10 §6). Not concealed; simply somewhere nobody goes to look, worked by the people who already live there. Which closes the loop on recruitment: the Star-Bound take from the far postings because the love has nothing there to spend itself on (D-79) **and because that is where the ships are.** The operators' own posting policy built the faction that left.

**Money.** The Kin have none that means anything (DOC-12 §5). The yards were funded substantially by **the Trust's descendants** (DOC-12 §6) — the foundation's heirs, embarrassed by the Program and proud of the Kin. **The people who specified the eyes, the fur and the love of Bach paid for the ships that took the Kin away.** It is atonement and it is one more thing decided *for* them by people with money and a doctrine, and the Kin know both halves and take the ship.

**Permission.** There was none and none was needed. The Moratorium forbids *making species*; **no law forbids building a ship**, and persons have been free to build things since AF 131. The operators objected on economics — twelve hundred of the best vacuum workers in the system walking out — and that is the argument that actually happened. Stopping them was politically unpayable thirty-nine years after the Shielded Storm: *you cannot be the operator who tells the Kin they may not go.* **It was legal, contested, and finished before the argument was. The Compact opened a file. The file is still open. The ship left.**

**Who opposed it.** The Anchorites' ancestors, on proximity — and, harder, **the Weavers** (D-110). Twelve hundred Kin leaving is twelve hundred lineages of Readings walking out of the carried record; to the faction whose position is *a memory no one carries is not a memory*, **an ark is an amputation of the archive.** They fought *Long Reach* and lost, and a century and a half later built *Quiet Carry* — same premise, opposite conclusion, one fire in between. They say both sentences at Weaves and do not pretend they are the same sentence.

*The yards themselves are unnamed — placeholders, house style, yours to fill.*

---
Six arks left Sol between AF 236 and 420, at ~3 % of lightspeed — a light-year every thirty-three years — for real stars within twelve light-years. The Kin do not need planets; they need a star to orbit, rock and ice to build with, and sunlight, so a debris disk is worth more than an Earth. Because Aethela do not age they need no generational replacement; they need strategies against **mental stagnation** and **memory loss**, and every ark answers those differently.

- **Transit torpor:** During transit 90 % of an ark's Kin sleep — Cortical Brain down, Bio-Core awake at 2–8 W hosting shared **Deep-Mesh Virtualities** — a *held dream* at ark scale (DOC-13 §1H, D-81): DEEP consented before torpor, the Core the medium, the *cortices* at dream-level composing, retelling, solving for years. The Core hosts; it does not compose (D-13). This is *not* vacuum torpor (State B, unconscious, 3 W — DOC-01E.2); a transit sleeper can be woken.
- **The Anchor Cluster:** At least one Cluster awake on a five-year rotation, maintaining reactor and hull (by bio-RADAR and hull-hearing), and — since no Vault exists — **carrying**: Readings for deaths in transit, Tellings before each rotation sleeps. Five awake Kin carrying a ship's dead is thin; whether sleepers are woken for a Reading is an ark-by-ark custom (*Quiet Carry* always does; *Far Nest* no longer does).
- **Arks make epics:** hundreds of sleepers retelling among themselves for decades converge on formula. Ark lineages carry consolidated, liturgical memory; a reactor procedure three centuries out is a liturgy, and liturgy can be wrong (DOC-01C §8). Humans are ten hops deep and already legend.
- **Long Messages:** every few decades, home — star charts, art, and retellings in Kin-code, taken on at Sol as *received* memory; since AF 480, received from the dead is carried. News travels outward too: *Held Breath* and *Quiet Carry* learned of *Long Reach* in flight, and their doctrines shifted mid-crossing.
- **No Kin medicine, no manuals, no Vault:** an ark carries everything it will ever know in the bodies aboard it.
- **The second cradle:** *Second Light* has been a colony for 136 years. Its messages describe habitats, not a voyage; a generation has been born there under two suns, and — with no human within four light-years — they are the first Kin for whom the creators were legend from birth. What Sol owes them, and they Sol, is the live question of the age.

## 3. KIN-NEST INTERIOR DESIGN
- **Communal Sleep-Webs:** A Cluster sleeps in one tension-suspended woven hammock — **the Nest** — in storm posture, tails interwoven, paws braced against each other, in a shared theta lock over near-field 1.5 GHz. Hours of isometric load a night: the Nest is also how bone is kept in zero-G, and a Kin who sleeps alone pays for it in bone (DOC-01A §4).
- **Cluster scent:** The Nest, the webbing and the walls carry the Cluster's contact-scent; a Silence Chamber is padded with it (DOC-01F §4).
- **Tactile Wall Art:** Molded acoustic and dielectric tiles, centimetres in relief, returning bio-RADAR pulses as invisible "spatial wallpaper."
- **Children:** raised in the Nest by every adult; where there are humans, with humans as neighbours and close friends. Anchorite Nests are near human sectors on purpose (DOC-02B §4–5).
