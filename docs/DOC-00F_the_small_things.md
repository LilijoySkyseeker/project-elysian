# PROJECT ELYSIAN: COMPREHENSIVE WORLD & SPECIES ARCHIVE
## Document ID: DOC-00F — The Small Things: a shuffle-deck of Kin–human social detail
**Codename:** Project Elysian | **Species:** Aethela (*Homo Sapiens Successor*) | **Self-Name:** The Kin
**Classification:** Tool, not canon. Every entry is *derived* from a mechanism that is canon; **using one in a scene does not rule it**, and a scene that contradicts one is not wrong.
**Status:** v1.0 — 2026-09-23. Written out of a defect: six scenes reached for the same three details.

---

### 0. WHY THIS EXISTS

`DOC-12 §7` is the whole bench, and it is lopsided:

- **Wrong** — ears as emotion · chairs · the kitchen · *write it up* · sizing the shelter · the margin on the tether · *pets* · posting one alone. **Eight.**
- **Right** — the pause · the hand on the chest · running the fan · a human *hand* · standing at a Reading · writing the log. **Six.**

A scene needs *right* far more often than *wrong*, because **this human is one of the good ones** is the commonest thing a scene has to establish — and three of those six fit anywhere, so those three got used every time. The bench is not shallow in general. **It is shallow in the one slot that gets drawn from most.**

This document is that slot, widened. Shuffle it. Take one.

---

### 1. HOW TO USE IT

1. **One competence marker per scene.** Not three. If the human is good, show it once and trust the reader.
2. **Never the same marker twice in the collection.** The deck is large enough now that it does not have to repeat. Check §9 before drawing.
3. **Pick by register, not by availability** — that is what went wrong. The tags:

| Tag | Register | What it does to a scene |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **W** | Warm competence | *This human is one of the good ones.* The overdrawn slot. |
| **C** | Comic | Affection through error. Usually the human, sometimes the Kin. |
| **P** | Professional / cold | Correct conduct without warmth. Operators, reviewers, courts. |
| **E** | Eerie | The reminder that they are not people in a suit. Use sparingly and never twice in a scene. |
| **R** | Rude | Ranges from thoughtless to obscene. Costs something. |

4. **Mark it spent** in §9 when a scene uses it.

### 10. ADDING TO IT

Two rules, and they are the same two the name pool has.

**Derive, do not invent.** Every entry above comes from a mechanism already in canon — a sense, a range, a rate, a rite. An entry with no mechanism under it is a flourish and will not survive a scene that leans on it.

**Add in kind.** Small, physical, unremarked, and *not explained in the scene that uses it*. The test is whether a reader who has never read a design document would take it as a fact about people rather than a fact about a species.
