### 1. DESIGN PREMISE — OURS, PLUS THE UPGRADES AN ENGINEER COULDN'T RESIST

The Kin were made to live alongside humans and to perceive the world the way humans do — the designer quirk (DOC-04) is only possible if a Kin sees the colour and hears the harmony a human does. So every sense starts from the human specification. Then the designers, who could not help themselves, improved each in the small ways an engineer improves anything: a wider aperture here, a fourth receptor there, an extra octave. Nothing a human would call a superpower; everything a human would notice as *sharper than me*. And where cute could be had for free — large eyes, enormous ears — it was taken.

Every upgrade has a cost, paid in the same currency each time: **a station is loud in every sense, and the Kin hear, smell and feel all of it.** Kin sectors are dark, quiet and scent-clean not as a style but as relief.

The radio senses (bio-RADAR, the mesh) are specified in DOC-01D and DOC-01C and are not repeated here, except where the ears' three jobs collide (§5D).

### 2. SIGHT (D-27)

| Parameter | Specification | Human | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Eye diameter | $\sim 35\text{ mm}$ | 24 mm | Large for the head: cute, and free low-light |
| Max pupil | $\sim 14\text{ mm}$ | 8 mm | $\sim 3\times$ light gathering ($\sim 1.6$ stops) |
| Photoreceptors | Human trichromat set **+ a fourth cone** peaking $\sim 500\text{ nm}$ (between blue and green) | 3 cones | Finer hue discrimination in the human gamut; colour vision is human-*compatible*, not alien |
| Tapetum | None | — | A tapetum blurs; the designers chose acuity over cat-grade night vision and let aperture do the work |
| Flicker fusion | $\sim 120\text{ Hz}$ | ~60 Hz | Human displays visibly flicker to a Kin; Kin-sector displays run fast |
| Dynamic range | $\sim 2$ stops wider than human, faster adaptation | | Sun-to-shadow in a bay without blinking |
| Acuity | $\sim 20/15$ | 20/20 | Modest |
| Protection | Dual nictitating membranes (DOC-01E §3): inner fluorocarbon moisture seal, outer gold/melanin electrochromic filter OD > 6 | | Humans on a feed call the filmed eye "silver" |

**What the eyes are for.** Not navigation — the Echo Cortex does that in the dark. The eyes are for **humans and human things**: faces, paintings, screens, sunlight, a cello's varnish. A Kin in a Kin sector may go a shift without using them. The pairing with RADAR is complementary (DOC-01D §4): optics give colour and texture, RADAR gives geometry and what is behind the wall. Losing an eye (DOC-01A §2A) costs a Kin the *human* half of the world for years.

### 3. NEAR-FIELD SENSE — THE PROXIMITY PIT NODES (D-27)

Sub-dermal nodes along the shoulders, dorsal line and flanks (DOC-01 §3), of the same electrocyte/electroreceptor tissue family as the Antenna Nuclei (DOC-01C §2): the shark and electric-fish lineage.

* **Principle:** passive and active **electrostatic / capacitive near-field** sensing. The nodes hold a small standing charge and read its distortion by nearby mass — conductor or dielectric, bulkhead or body. No medium is required, so **it works in vacuum.**
* **Range:** roughly the size of the object — $\sim 1\text{ m}$ for a bulkhead or a body, $\sim 0.2\text{ m}$ for a hand tool. Resolution is coarse: *something, this big, closing at this rate, from there.*
* **Percept:** a pressure-like sense with no human word — Vesper-7's "pleasant buzz" (S002) as a conduit passes over her back. It is the sense of *what is about to touch me*, and it is why Kin move through crowded rails without looking.
* **Cost:** strong static discharges (the reason for the conductive-pad grounding of DOC-01B §6) blind it briefly; a badly grounded human sector *itches*.
* **In vacuum:** fully functional, and the only near-field sense left.

### 4. SMELL (D-28)

**Strength:** dog-level — $\sim 10^4\times$ human sensitivity — through a large turbinate mass that is also the evaporative cooling loop (DOC-01B §8). A good nose and a good radiator are the same organ.

**What it is tuned to (receptor targets chosen by the designers):**

| Target | Why | Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Station hazards** — hydrazine, ammonia coolant, ozone, burning polymer, CO, outgassing solvents | A biological gas detector on every watch | parts-per-billion (ammonia $\sim 0.5\text{ ppb}$ vs human ~5 ppm) |
| **Human state** — sweat chemistry: stress, fear, illness, fatigue, sleep debt | The Pet/Protector channel; a vomeronasal-class organ tuned to *us* | Sub-conscious; a Kin knows a human is not fine before the human says so |
| **Trace metals** — Cu, Zn, Na in food | The diet (DOC-01B §4) as flavour: metals *taste good* | — |
| **Cluster scent** | The social sense that survives The Silence | — |

**Scent behaviour.** There are **no marking glands** — the designers deleted territorial marking as a product decision. Scent is shared *by contact*: sebaceous secretions in the pelt (the same wax-ester layer that seals in vacuum, DOC-01E §3B) carry an individual signature, and the constant body-to-body contact of Kin life — the Nest, the tail-twine, the casual lean — leaves a light **Cluster scent** on each other and on their surroundings. A Kin sector smells, faintly, of its Cluster. A Silence Chamber is padded with it.

**Personal space.** The Kin do not hold the human notion of it. Touch is not an event to them; it is the resting state. Humans who live among Kin discover, usually within a week, that they have become *surroundings* — leaned on, brushed past, tail-touched, scent-marked in the mildest possible sense — and that this was never a decision anyone made.

**Cost.** Human sectors are *loud*: two hundred people's sweat, cooking, solvents. A human's smell is intimacy or intrusion depending on who. Kin sectors are kept scent-clean by habit and filtration.

**In vacuum:** anosmic — the nasal sphincters seal in the Voiding Cascade (DOC-01E §1). Smell is the first sense back on repressurisation; the first breath after an EVA is the smell of home.

### 5. HEARING & VOICE (D-29)

#### A. Hearing
| Parameter | Specification | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Range | $\sim 20\text{ Hz}$ – $\sim 50\text{ kHz}$ | Human band at full musical fidelity, extended two octaves |
| Directionality | $\sim 1^\circ$ in azimuth with pinnae converged | Large mobile pinnae on a $\sim 20\text{ cm}$ baseline |
| Sensitivity | $\sim 10\text{ dB}$ better than human across the band | |
| Music | Human harmonic perception intact (designer quirk); more overtones audible → a taste for rich timbres (the cello of S004) | |

**Leak-hearing.** Gas escaping a fitting hisses in the ultrasonic; a Kin hears a pinhole leak at $\sim 40\text{ kHz}$ across a bay. With the nose at ppb, a Kin on watch is a walking leak detector — the on-page reason Sorrel is in the coolant bay (S005).

**Cost.** Pumps, fans, bearing whine; human klaxons are painful. Kin alerting is ambient RF (DOC-03 §1), never sound. Kin sectors are quiet.

#### B. The private channel
Whistle-tones above $\sim 20\text{ kHz}$ are inaudible to humans and audible to Kin at $50\text{ kHz}$. Two Kin in a room full of people can talk *acoustically* — low bandwidth, a few tens of bits per second, but private, and working when RF is gone. Not what the designers intended; exactly what the Kin do. Humans who learn of it find it rude. The Kin, asked, say it is the same as two humans exchanging a look.

#### C. Voice
* **Larynx** — human-range speech through the cranial resonating chambers (DOC-01 §2); the Kin speak human languages with an acoustic warmth humans notice and cannot place.
* **Dual whistle organ** — syrinx-like, two independent sources, to $\sim 30\text{ kHz}$; the two soaring whistle-lines of the choral tradition (DOC-04). A whistle overtone can ride *under* speech as an emotional colour — humans hear it as "musical" without knowing why; Kin hear it as the speaker's state.
* **Range** — the wide acoustic vocal range DOC-01 promises: a low human-like register, a whistle register above it, and a Kin voice can carry both at once.

#### D. The ears' three jobs
The pinnae are radiators (DOC-01B §8), phased arrays (DOC-01D) and acoustic pinnae. They cannot serve all three at once:

| Posture | Serves | Costs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Converged on a sound | Hearing | RADAR beam off-axis; radiator area toward the sound |
| Steered for RADAR | Bio-RADAR / mesh | Hearing off-axis |
| Fanned wide | Heat dump | Neither aimed |
| Folded flat | Heat retention (vacuum, cold) | Nearly deaf; RADAR narrowed |

Humans read ear position as emotion. **Half the time it is thermal management.** Sorrel's ears flaring "when thinking" is a real tell — the Echo Cortex and Lattice warming under load — but a Kin fanning her ears at you in a hot bay is not interested, just hot. Kin learn early which humans can tell the difference.

### 6. TOUCH & HULL-HEARING (D-29)

* **Pads and hands:** human-grade tactile acuity in the hands; paw pads coarser but load-sensing (the anchor budget of DOC-01A §3 is *felt*).
* **Whiskers:** vibrissae on the muzzle and above the eyes — near-field airflow and contact in the dark. In vacuum they report only contact.
* **Hull-hearing.** In vacuum there is no sound, but a Kin locked to the rails feels **structure-borne vibration** through pads and tail — pumps, impacts, a hull working under thermal load, a pressure door cycling three frames away. The pit-node tissue family reads it. A Kin on EVA does not just fix the hull; she listens to it, and comes back in with an opinion about the number-two pump.

### 7. SENSE STATE BY ENVIRONMENT

| Sense | Cabin | Kin sector (dark, quiet, clean) | Vacuum | The Silence (RF-shielded room) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Bio-RADAR / mesh | full | full | full | **gone** |
| Sight | full | mostly idle | full (membranes closed; gold filter in sun) | full |
| Near-field | full | full | full | full |
| Smell | full (loud) | full (clean) | **gone** | full — Cluster scent is the comfort |
| Hearing | full (loud) | full (quiet) | hull-hearing only | full — the private whistle channel still works |
| Touch | full | full | full | full |

A Kin in vacuum is a creature of geometry, near-field and vibration. A Kin in The Silence is, for the first time, a creature of the human senses only — which is part of why it is unbearable.
