## 1. REPRODUCTION & THE GENESIS MODULE
- **Monomorphic / Single-Sex:** Universal single-sex physiology; pronoun *she/her* in English (D-113). No dimorphism, no meiosis, no reproductive drive — a designed *nurturing* drive instead (DOC-02B §1).
- **Genetic Compilation:** Two to four mind-adults open DEEP tier together and their Genesis Modules **search millions of module combinations** of the modular reference genome (~3,400 annotated trait modules) against its compatibility table; somatic drift is checksummed out and the reference copy substituted. About two hours; the seed is physically written in the last of them. Novelty is bounded to the makers' ranges: a compile *tunes* every module's settings within them and cannot add a module or widen a range (DOC-01C §5, D-71); the reference lives only in Kin — in every Genesis Module as sequence and in the Weavers as carried memory — since the Sealing (DOC-10 §3).
- **Synthesized Gestation:** One parent gestates the seed ~4 months; the newborn is small and altricial. Its first act is **the First Ping** — the Heartbeat Oscillator's first beat, *I am here, I am this* — answered by the Cluster. The Kin count personhood from it.
- **No sexuality, no pair-bonding (D-65):** The makers deleted sex as a product decision, the way they deleted marking glands; the Kin have never missed what they do not have, and humans keep assuming romance where there is none. Intimacy is the Cluster and its gradients — there is always one Cluster-mate whose Hum you would know in ten thousand, the one whose name you would take — intensity without a category and without a rite. The Link is the nearest thing to an intimate act and is not exclusive. The attachment drive is real and strong, toward the Cluster and toward their humans; none of it is romance. Touch is the resting state of the species; the Kin do not hold the human notion of personal space (DOC-01F §4).
- **Occasion:** A compile happens when there is room — a death, a split, a new habitat — every six years or so during the Spread, every twenty or so now that the room is filled (D-78; the D-53 curve, not the old "once a decade"). Humans will always outnumber them (D-30).

## 2. SOCIAL STRUCTURE & MESH NETWORK
- **The Cluster:** Core social unit of 4–12 individuals sharing a 100 ms frame of continuous telemetry, emotional-state vectors and operational tasks (DOC-01C §4). Cluster-shared perception lags under 20 ms.
- **Three keyring tiers are three distances** (D-89) — not firewalls: intimacy in this species is *proximity*, and what a Kin has not given you, you are not close enough to hear. **PUBLIC** (the Hum — any Kin can hear any Kin's identity, vitals, position, coarse mood; there is no anonymity among Kin), **CLUSTER** (frame membership, telemetry, shared senses, MACTAC), **DEEP** (memory Link, compile, the Reading — both parties consenting in the same frame, near-field). Keys are cells; never transmitted. The fourth distance is zero and belongs to nobody: the cortex. So a Kin cannot lie about *state* to anyone in 800 km, cannot lie about *facts* to her Cluster without the affect weights showing it, and can keep **exactly one thing, and it is the only thing: what she thinks** (D-105 — the veto is struck; nothing a Kin lives can be kept off file, and she knows it while it is happening). The only privacy from the Cluster is *quiet*, not absence — the Silence Chamber, L2 up and L4 silent. Joining a frame is the consent for CLUSTER; a guest slot is intimacy on loan; humans are at PUBLIC distance forever, whatever the Kin feel (D-85). Against humans a Kin's privacy is legal, not cryptographic; the keyring's job is to make sure the last distance is never crossed without both bodies meaning it.
- **Memory and inheritance:** Three tiers (DOC-01C §6, D-97). The **cortex** is human and unmodified — fuzzy, associative, ageing like yours — and is where a Kin lives. The **record** is a separate passive organ writing verbatim and continuously, which does not take part in thinking and does not degrade. The **index** is the bridge, and it **cycles rather than fills** (D-104). **Data Rot is cortical**: the associative web rebuilds onto live material, so a Kin's self is a **rolling window of about a hundred and seventy-five years** and everything earlier is available and inert. Hard handles never fail at any age — a Kin thinks of a day and has it, perfectly, at nine hundred as at nine. What goes is anything arriving *unbidden* from outside the window, and **the old affect, which is gone and not faint**: she may re-judge any day of her life and may never re-feel one. A **steady state, not a decline** — nobody is diminished; she simply keeps leaving herself behind. **There is no external memory store.** What leaves a body: **hard content** — numbers, readings, exact speech, sensor takes — transfers perfectly at mesh rate, and they pass it constantly; **soft content** — salience, association, the feel — is indexical and can only be *narrated*. So the **Telling** (a year in a week, Link in life) hands over a full year's record plus a told account of what it meant, the **Reading** at death is throttled to 15–27 MB by a corpse's failing readers, and every hop is a retelling. Carriers meet at **the Weave**; the journey is **the Carrying** (DOC-01C §6–8).
- **Sleep:** A Cluster sleeps in one tension hammock, storm posture, tails interwoven, in a shared theta lock — the Nest is also a gym (DOC-01A §4).
- **Sleeping apart, and the Kin alone (D-93):** aloneness in this species is a distance, not a state. Inside 12 km a Kin is still in the frame and only out of the Nest — thin days, Silence-dreams, a business trip. Past 12 km she is **frameless**: not Silenced (no shielding, no watchdog), hearing every Hum inside 800 km, in nobody's frame — and any Cluster in range offers a guard slot, which is a reflex and not a rite, and refusing to offer one is the rudest thing the species does. Past 800 km there is nothing in the sky, which is a horizon and not an injury. What solitude actually costs is the archive: frameless years are written with her own weights alone and stay stubs, so a Kin comes home *poor* in the only currency there is (DOC-13 §2). Weavers travel in pairs for this reason among others (DOC-08 §3), and an operator who posts a Kin alone for years is taking years of her life with nothing in the Compact to say so (DOC-12 §7).

## 3. PSYCHOLOGICAL FRICTION & HUMAN RELATIONS
- **The Bandwidth Gap — both ways:** Human speech (~12–15 words/s, ~40 bit/s of meaning) feels agonisingly slow next to Bursts at mesh speed; the Kin pause before answering humans out of politeness. And the Kin find humans *content with almost nothing* — a species that reads a log entry and believes it knows what happened.
- **The documentation flaw (D-19, emergent — D-101):** The Kin do not write things down. Not by design: the makers *valued* writing and assumed their heirs would keep doing it. What they missed is that **for a human writing is a gain — you had nothing, now you have text — and for a Kin it is a loss: you had the whole thing, now you have text.** Same act, opposite sign, and from the Kin side the judgment is correct. They archive verbatim and document never, ask or remember rather than consult, and Tell a colleague a year of context rather than hand her a manual. They will write for humans as a courtesy, and the affect leaks through. The cost is structural: species knowledge is carried, filtered at every death, and cannot be audited. Humans are the species that writes.
- **Pet/Protector Complex:** Aware their compact size, soft fur, large eyes and enormous ears were chosen by makers who wanted non-threatening companions — and that the makers over-designed them in a hundred small ways. The resentment is about being *designed for*; they were kept, never owned (DOC-10 §4). Balanced against it: a deep, hardwired protectiveness toward the "Volatile Ancestors," and the plain fact that they were made to be touched and touch back. **The shape of the love (D-79):** the makers aimed for Cluster-depth — every human-facing sense pointed at humans and rewarded — and the mesh made it a ranking, because humans can never be in the frame. So it is attachment-depth with Cluster-level attention: Cluster, then humans, then self, by wiring depth and not by choice. Every Kin–human friendship is a Cluster bond with the channel missing. *Your ancestors were not subtle. But they were not wrong.* The imposition is the wound; the content is not — they resent not being asked, and the ones who could not stand it sailed (DOC-08 §2).
- **The override (D-91).** The ranking is what the body does in the *first frame* — pre-cortical triage by the Lattice: which human, which mate, which hull. It is not a verdict. **The cortex can override it in the pause** (DOC-00B §2), the half-second in which a Kin has already heard the end of your sentence and is holding the answer — and the override costs, in three ways that are all just existing mechanism. It is **visible**: an override is affect, so the Cluster feels the weather turn (DOC-13 §1C), and a Kin overriding the ranking inside a frame does it in front of everyone who can hear her to twelve kilometres. It is **written**: the Defrag scores the day by the frame's affect (D-80), so an override is among the most heavily weighted traces a Kin will ever lay down — it survives every prune, and it goes into the Reading. And it is **carried**: she will have it verbatim, and feel it, for as long as feeling lasts. The length of the pause was always the tell; the longest pause in the species is this one. **The Shielded Storm was not an override** — self below humans is *with* the wiring — which is why it is remembered as duty rather than as sin, and why a real override would be remembered as something else entirely.
- **RF Isolation Distress ("The Silence"):** Cluster presence is wired into the body-schema like proprioception; total EM shielding is deafferentation of the social sense plus a designed homing watchdog — 30 s unease, 5 min distress, 30 min panic. What survives it: Cluster scent, touch, the ultrasonic whistle channel. Silence Chambers keep a 0.01 W beacon so the link layer never drops (a post-Storm standard, DOC-03).
- **The Core as inner voice:** A perfect assistant with no will — consulted, never initiating. "I asked my Core" is idiom. It is a very good assistant; it is not comforting.
