## 1. The want
Teasel wants to be no trouble. **Under that:** hir is hungry all the time and has been for three weeks, and hir is hiding it. Since Haw came home with hir arm bound, Haw needs more (a limb regrowing costs calories every day for years, and a fracture costs more now). The arithmetic Sloe does every night doesn't close. So Teasel has been **quietly moving hir own share into Haw's**: an egg left in the straw by Haw's head, walnuts slipped into Haw's pile when Sloe isn't counting. The frame carries hir hunger to all four of them as a low, steady wrongness, and none of them can find its cause. Hir says *"I'm fine"* and *"Fine"* and, lately, nothing. **Wrong about:** that hiding it protects them. They feel it anyway.

## 2. The shape
- **Week three, the barn.** Show the arithmetic from Teasel's side:
  - Sloe counting at dusk;
  - Yarrow's six bits a day, half of it gone to Applejack before hir sees it;
  - Haw eating everything and asking for more, cheerful, the arm bound across hir chest;
  - the egg Teasel leaves by Haw's head.
  **Teasel notices what everyone eats**, and that is hir lens on the world (VOICES), for the whole chapter. Hir is thin, cold at night, and slow on the river road. Madder asks *[Teasel. Status]* and gets *[Fine]*.
- **The Crusaders come to the barn** (about day fifteen). **They come by themselves, not sent**, to say it in their own words, which is what Rarity took from Sweetie in ch. 7. Applejack walked them to the road and waits there, out of earshot, which Teasel notices. The three stand in the doorway. Then:
  - **On the radio, the five are loud and the barn is silent.** The fillies see only five strangers gone still. Teasel hears everything, several threads at once:
    - **Madder** is cold (*[Facts are logged. Nothing here changes the rota]*);
    - **Sloe** manages (*say something kind, they're children, look at them*);
    - **Haw** laughs, a real laugh, arm bound (three children and a mirror, and here they all are);
    - and then **Yarrow**, clean: ***[I knew. The wagon. The day it came out of the burn]***. That lands on the other four at once: Yarrow kept it for ten days. Madder's affect goes hard.
  - **Aloud, Madder asks the fillies the one question that matters to a Kin: *what was it like?*** Facts are cheap; the account, the feel of it, is the costly thing (DOC-00D §7, D-103). Rarity gave them the facts; only the fillies can give this. The fillies hear it as cold.
  - **Scootaloo answers.** She tells what it was like, and **she tells the stick**: that she put a stick in the seam and it broke because of her. That's the gap Rarity felt in ch. 7, now in Scootaloo's own mouth. Apple Bloom and Sweetie Belle add their pieces. It's a children's account, uneven, and it costs them. The Kin can hear the cost in their voices and smell it on them.
  - **Apple Bloom sees the ribbon on Teasel's wrist.** Teasel unties it with hir teeth and a thumb, and holds it out. It's small and awkward, and **not a symbol**. Apple Bloom takes it after a moment. It's ruined, and she puts it in her saddlebag, not in her mane. Through the frame, Sloe feels the only trade good they had go, and says nothing.
  - **The five do not agree about what to say back**, and **nobody forgives anybody**. Madder says something formal, which the fillies can't read. Sloe says too much. Haw is still laughing. Yarrow says nothing. **Teasel says one true small thing aloud** to the fillies about that night, a piece of hir own account (the frog, or the dead animal, or the smell of them running), and it costs hir, and the frame feels hir go. The fillies leave. Applejack walks them home.
  - Afterwards, in code, it's Madder and Yarrow: short, hard, unfinished.
- **The hunger peaks (week four).** Teasel goes to the river more and more: to drink, and to be away from the frame's noise. Hir **smells the fish** in the river (Haw put a hand in, in week one). Hir asks the Core what a fish would be, as food: dense protein, zinc. It's the obvious answer, and hir has never killed anything. Hir remembers the frog, and the doe that ran into the smoke. **Fluttershy lives downstream, with her animals**, and Teasel knows it.
- **Rushlight** (VOICES: original to this story). An old earth mare, a retired river-barge hand, who lives in a cottage by **the weir below the mill**. She sees Teasel on the bank more than once, watching the water, and **says little**. She has worked the river ports down to Manehattan and has seen gryphons eat fish off the dock, and she is neither shocked nor fond of it. One evening she says, in river words, where **the deep pool below the weir** is, where the big ones lie up in the cold, and goes back indoors. **That's all she does. Teasel does the rest.**
- **After the middle: the river.** At the pool, at dusk:
  - Teasel's hands in the cold water, still for a long time. Kin hands are fast, and the grip locks.
  - **The fish, caught, alive, fighting.** Hir kills it, badly and then properly. Hir **eats it raw, on the bank**, the acid gut built for exactly this. **Plain, not lingered on, not sacred, not a speech.**
  - The frame feels the spike (the killing) and then something else hir can't strip. **The social cost:** hir is seen. A mare and two foals on the towpath see a Kin on the bank with a fish in hir hands and blood on hir mouth; the mare turns them round, and somebody says *gryphon* like a word you don't say. By the next day the town has it. Teasel knows it will.
- **Life goes on at four weeks.** Glimpses, all through Teasel's eyes and hir lens (**what people are eating**), short, no lessons:
  - Pinkie at the bakery's back door at five with flour to the elbow, eating a heel of bread standing up;
  - Twilight on the library steps with a sandwich going soft, a new card box under her wing;
  - Rarity at the boutique window with tea she hasn't drunk;
  - Spike polishing something, paid now or not;
  - Dash on the crew line again;
  - Applejack's tab, and the running total, lower;
  - rain some days;
  - **a rota nobody keeps**;
  - Fluttershy, once, far off, with a basket, who does not come to the barn and does not not come.
- **Ending: an image, no comment, and better. Teasel has eaten.** The drafter chooses the image; it must *show* that hir has eaten, and that it is better, without saying either. For example: the heap at night, where the low, steady thing in the frame that four people have felt for three weeks is not there, and none of them know why yet; or Teasel on the bank at the pool at first light, rinsing hir hands in the river, with the forest smelling of rain. **Stop on the image.** Better, not perfect: nothing is fixed, and the fish will cost them in town.

## 3. What is lost
The ribbon (the only thing they had to trade, given back). Some of the town's tolerance (the fish). Teasel's secret. Yarrow's standing with Madder. And one thing gained: Teasel has eaten.

## 4. Voice card
**Teasel** (VOICES.md) in the narration: correct, quiet, observant, and **less and less talkative**. Hir notices **what other people are eating** and **smells** (rain on the forest, the river, bread hir can't digest, the fillies' fear). Hir humour is shy and dry, and surprising when it comes. Hir deflects. Hir is not a waif to be rescued, and is not saved by a pony's kindness. First of hir line, with no number, two years on the crew, and still proving hirself.
**The Crusaders:** real kids. **Apple Bloom** (bossy, and hurt about her sister); **Sweetie Belle** (runs on, then stops dead, and asks the question everyone was avoiding); **Scootaloo** (tough, clipped, and goes quiet when scared, then tells the stick). **Not** the "Cutie Mark Crusaders!" cry, not cutie-mark talk, not a chorus of *"we're sorry"*.
**Rushlight:** few words, all practical, river slang. Never asks personal questions. **Not** the wise mentor, not the grandmother, not the one who fixes it.
**Madder** (cold, exact, and angry at Yarrow), **Sloe** (manages, too many words), **Haw** (arm bound, laughing, hungry, delighted), **Yarrow** (says the one thing, clean).

## 5. Channels (VOICES.md governs; DOC-00D is canon)

The body: ears, tail, touch in the heap, involuntary sounds. **Ponies say *she* by now** (VOICES), mostly. **Teasel's clean sends are a tell**; the others know it.
