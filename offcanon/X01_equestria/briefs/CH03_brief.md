# BRIEF — X01 chapter 3 *(working title: "The Yard")*
**Outline:** OUTLINE §3. **POV:** Fluttershy, close third, past tense. **Target:** about 3,500 words. **Span:** first light to early afternoon, day one.
**Status:** brief → predicted → drafted → linted → cold read + fidelity read → revised → owner

## 1. The want
Fluttershy wants her animals safe and her morning's work done, and she wants these things out of her yard. **Under that, and at war with it,** she wants to help, because they are plainly lost and plainly frightened, and she can't *not* see that.

## 2. The obstacle / what goes wrong after the middle
- **Obstacle:** five sixty-kilo, predator-faced strangers at the forest edge at dawn, smelling of smoke. Her animals panic before she's properly awake: the hens (who had stopped dead on the ramp, per ch. 2) break and scatter, rabbits bolt, birds go up. She is frightened and **angry**, with reason, and her work does not stop: dozens of animals to feed, check and settle, around the strangers.
- **After the middle:** one of the young rabbits bolts **toward the smoke** and doesn't come back. She goes to the tree line after it and can't go further; the Everfree is the Everfree, and there is smoke in it. Then, near noon, **Rainbow Dash** arrives on weather-team business (a fire at the forest edge; the team's being called; keep the animals in) and finds five of *them* in Fluttershy's yard. Dash is on the clock and on a reprimand, so she is **by the book**. The strangers are a **work problem**, not a fight: she takes out her notes and asks them what she'd ask any witness at a fire (where it started, when, which way it's moving). **Madder answers exactly**: times to the second, bearings, the ring round where they woke, the strip downhill. It unnerves Dash more than any threat would. Then Dash tells Fluttershy the crew will be working this whole edge by afternoon, and the strangers can't be here. That is the pressure that forces Fluttershy's closing line. **Dash never takes a fighting stance, and Fluttershy never has to defend them to her** (predicted).

## 3. What is lost
The rabbit. And the good spring morning she had been having: nests everywhere, a fox kit she raised back with kits of her own.

## 4. Voice card
**Fluttershy** (VOICES.md) in the narration:
- Her work is skilled, practical and unglamorous. With animals she is firm, quick and exact. The narration knows every animal by name and state at a glance, and that is how *her* mind orders the world.
- With strangers she is frightened, and under it angry. The anger comes out **small and hard**, not loud.
- **Not** the healer who sees past the monster. **Not** the first friend. **Not** mistaking them for foxes that need care (predicted). She sees predators, big and wrong, and she's right that that's what they are built like.
**Rainbow Dash:** professional on the clock (which surprises people), fast, sharp, protective of Fluttershy. She's on a reprimand for showing off, and it shows in how carefully by-the-book she is being: notes, questions, no heroics. **Not** the race or challenge, **not** "awesome", **not** impressed, **not** a fighting stance, **not** "whoa, what ARE those".

## 5. The Kin, from outside
Fluttershy does **not** hear their radio. What she sees:
- **They go still all at once**, and turn their heads at the same instant, as if someone had spoken.
- **Ears:** hers are the only ears she knows how to read, and she reads theirs wrong (they fan wide in the sun; she takes it for aggression).
- **They stay in contact**: a hand on a back, tails over each other.
- **They are useless with animals**: fascinated, far too close, far too still, then far too sudden. They have never met an animal. **Haw** tries to help with the hens and makes it much worse.
- **When they speak to her,** it is aloud, careful and slow, every word placed, with a small pause before they answer (the first time she notices it, it frightens her more). Human first names: once she says her name, they use it. They are polite and tired, and plainly hungry. **Their speech is careful and plain, never stilted-formal**: no "We did not mean harm" or "We mean no harm", and no "What are they? Are they people?" about the animals (predicted). What they ask is practical and specific, and it's often the wrong question.
- **One of them has a scorched red ribbon on hir wrist** (Sloe). It **nags** at Fluttershy, and she **doesn't place it**: she's frightened, it's burned, it's on the wrong creature. Leave it unplaced (continuity: the Crusaders' secret holds).
- **Madder** does the talking at first, with short, clear requests. **Sloe** takes over, warmer and too many words. **Teasel** stays back, and looks at what the animals are eating.

## 6. Shape
- Past tense, close third on Fluttershy.
- **Ending: a line of dialogue, torn** (the owner's instruction). Fluttershy's closing line is an **offer and a boundary at once**: kindness that is not a welcome. She wants to help and needs them gone; they are in the way and not helping. **Slightly more positive than a refusal.** Stop on it. **Banned wordings** (predicted): *not yet*, *not inside*, *the door*, *by the fence*, *by the gate*, *water by…*, *you can stay…*. Make it concrete and hers: something tied to her day's work and to where they could go that isn't here.
- Tone: angry, working, torn.

## 7. Lore budget
The Kin's mechanics are seen only as behaviour (§5), never explained: no radio, frame or Core named from her side. **Equestria as backdrop:** Fluttershy's cottage (the grassy roof, the birdhouses, the hen house), her animals, the Everfree's edge, and the weather team as a job (the Everfree's weather runs itself, so the crew can only work the edge). **No episode is retold.** Angel may appear as one rabbit among many, **not** as a comic glare (predicted in ch. 2's list).
