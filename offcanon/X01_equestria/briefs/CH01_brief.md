## 1. The want
Apple Bloom wants to bring home something that is hers: something she found, that nobody gave her and nobody can take off her. It's been a bad week at home and she doesn't know why.

## 2. The obstacle / what goes wrong after the middle
- **Obstacle:** the mirror is heavy, the light is going, it's the Everfree, and her friends want different things. **Not one line per filly echoing her mentor** (predicted): Sweetie Belle wants to leave it because it gives her a bad feeling she can't explain; Scootaloo wants to crack the frame open and see what's inside, because she's bored of carrying it; Apple Bloom wants it in the barn, under a tarp, *hers*.
- **After the middle:** it wakes. For a few seconds it shows other places, one after another, fast and wrong. Then it grabs something bright and holds on, and it comes apart. Heat, fire, and a heap of shapes on the path where there was nothing. They run, and they leave the wagon.

## 3. What is lost
The wagon (Big Mac built it for her birthday). The afternoon. And the thing she can't name yet: they did something enormous, and they ran.

## 4. Voice card
**Apple Bloom** (VOICES.md), in the narration. The prose takes on her habits (R03 §2.1):
- farm-kid practical: she measures things in chores, bushels and how far to the barn;
- bossy, and fast to decide;
- worried about Applejack's mood all week, and sure it's her fault;
- country turns of phrase, used sparingly: the narration is *hers*, not a dialect joke.
**Sweetie Belle** runs on, then stops dead, and asks the question everyone was avoiding. **Scootaloo** is tough and clipped, and goes quiet when scared. They bicker like real kids, meanly sometimes.

## 5. Shape
- Past tense, close third on Apple Bloom.
- **Ending type: cut mid-action.** Running. Stop while they're still running, before home, before anyone is told.
- Tone: fun and bickering, turning to fear. **Their panic is real and not comic** (BIBLE §1A).

## 6. Lore budget
- **Equestria (show canon, as backdrop only):** the ruined castle of the royal sisters in the Everfree; the forest is forbidden to them and they know it; the Everfree's weather and animals run themselves. **No episode is retold**, and no reference is made to what happened at the castle in the show.
- **The mirror:** old, cracked, heavy, frame of dark metal; they don't know what it is. It **wakes** on the way home. They don't activate it on purpose: a crack, a jolt of the wagon, a hoof on the glass. The glimpses of other places are **original** (no other franchise's worlds) and **not a montage or a list** (predicted). At most two, specific and odd; one of them has a sound. No humans, no oceans of stars, no city of glass.
- **The lock:** something bright, humming and *regulated*. Then the glass comes apart and something comes *through*: heat, a flash, fire in the undergrowth, and shapes.
- **The Kin, from outside:** vague, except for **one specific wrong thing** Apple Bloom sees clearly and can't stop seeing: **a hand, with a thumb**, closing on the edge of her wagon (Kin hand-paws, DOC-00B §1). Otherwise it's a dark heap, big, moving. **Not "so many legs", not clicking, not eyes glowing** (predicted). The fillies see almost nothing clearly, because of fire, wild magic, and *we messed up, we're so dead* (BIBLE §1A). **No Kin POV, no Kin words, no Kin lore.**
- **Absent:** cutie-mark talk as the *reason* for anything; the CMC battle cry; timberwolves; Zecora; any adult arriving; Rainbow Dash.

## 7. Details that demonstrate nothing (at least three)
1. Big Mac painted her name on the wagon's side for her birthday, and the B is backwards. Nobody has told him.
2. Sweetie Belle's saddlebag has half a daisy sandwich in it that she's been saving, and she eats it at the worst possible moment.
3. There's a beetle on the mirror frame, and Scootaloo won't touch the mirror until it's gone.
4. Apple Bloom's bow keeps snagging on low branches.
