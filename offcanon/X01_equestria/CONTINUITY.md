## The arrival night (chapters 1–2)
**The path:** an old track from the ruined castle down through the Everfree toward Ponyville. The ground falls to the **left** of the path (heading home) and rises to the right.
1. The Crusaders cross a **small brown stream in a dip** (ch. 1). The path then climbs a short rise.
2. **The arrival point** is on that rise, past the stream. The mirror lay face-up in the wagon bed.
3. **The plasma:** when the mirror broke, a flare of plasma came out of the glass. It lit the ferns in a **ring round the wagon** on both sides of the path. A **tongue of it rolled downslope to the left** and scorched a **strip** about a hundred metres long into a **small clearing**, where it burned out on thin ground. The small furred animal (a creature about the size of a squirrel, with a long flattened tail) died there. The ferns by the path **keep burning slowly**, spreading **uphill and to the right** with the night air. The fire is **behind and above** anyone who goes downhill.
4. **The fillies** fled left, downhill, **through the scorched strip into that clearing**. Scootaloo froze over the dead animal; Apple Bloom dragged her on, and they went down a steep bank toward water. They are gone before the Kin are fully awake, and the Kin only **hear and smell** small bodies running away, with a sharp fear-smell.
5. **The Kin wake** at the arrival point, in the burning ring. Yarrow's lower hand is locked on the wagon (grip reflex, asleep). The **mirror frame** (dark metal, empty, a few glass splinters) is left leaning on a tree there. The **feed sacks** are burned. **Apple Bloom's red bow** is caught in the harness buckle; Sloe keeps it on hir wrist.
6. **The Kin go downhill, left**, away from the fire, down the same scorched strip, so they reach **the dead animal first, early, close to the arrival point**. Then the ground falls to a **stream hollow** (a different stream, larger than the one the fillies crossed) with a flat-topped stone: the **frog**. They follow that stream down and out of the forest.
7. **Fluttershy's cottage** stands where that stream leaves the forest: a small plank bridge, a cottage built into a hill with a grassed roof, birdhouses everywhere, and a hen house with a ramp and a hatch.
8. **Timeline:** the arrival at last light (dusk). The Kin surface in a few minutes, move off within about twenty, and walk through the night on bodies that don't work under gravity (slow: hours). They reach the forest edge at **first light**. Chapter 3 begins at dawn at the cottage.
9. **What the Kin can read:** the wagon says **APPLE BLOOM** in red paint, in letters they can read (the ponies write in the same alphabet the Kin know). The B is backwards.

## Standing facts
- **Five Kin, all present:** Madder-4, Sloe-5, Yarrow-3, Haw-2, Teasel. Nothing came with them but the plasma. No tools, no food: they were asleep.
- **The ship is never shown or answered.**
