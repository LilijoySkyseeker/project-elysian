Four of them sent it at once, and Madder was one of the four.

*[What's burning]*

Haw was the fifth. She sent something else over the top of it.

*[I was falling fast ~ still falling, thrilled with it]*

Sloe sent from somewhere above her.

*[Your leg's on my back]*

Haw sent straight back.

*[Whose leg, which one, I've got six—]*

Yarrow sent.

*[Why is it heavy]*

Teasel was sending fast.

*[It's hot on the left. It's hot on the right. Madder, it's hot on—]*

Madder sent, from underneath.

*[Sloe, that's my arm you're lying on]*

*[I'm not lying on anything, something's lying on me ~ muffled, indignant, and under that frightened]*

Madder got an eye open, and saw it.

Flame. Not a red mark on a deck plan, not a drill: the thing itself, standing up out of the ferns an arm's length away, yellow and tearing, with nothing between it and them but air. All of them went rigid in the same instant.

Somebody whined.

Madder's ears had fanned wide against the heat before she knew she had them.

Then one word, from everyone.

*[FIRE ~ all of them, on top of each other]*

Sloe sent.

*[Where's suppression, where's the—]*

Madder was already sending.

*[Seal it, seal the—]*

Yarrow cut across them both.

*[There's no hatch. There's no wall. There's nothing to seal]*

They had gone down at the end of second watch, in the web by the coolant bay. They had come up in a heap, and nothing else was right. A heap in the web drifted. This one had gone flat like a sack put down, so that every body pressed hard into the one below. Madder was near the bottom, where Sloe's weight lay across her ribs. The ground under all of them was not a deck.

She put the checklist up. *Heads. Hands. Air. Exits.* She got as far as hands before she saw it. Right against the heap, touching it, stood a box on wheels. Yarrow's lower left hand was locked on its tailboard, closed there while she slept.

Then four other threads came in over the checklist, and she lost the place. Haw's was loudest.

*[There's a box. There's a box on wheels, Yarrow's holding a box on wheels]*

Two more tangled under it. Then Teasel sent, and hers came clean, with nothing under it at all.

*[Something ran]*

Madder swivelled both ears downhill. Something was running down there, small and fast and more than one. A smell came up the slope after it, sharp and sour like a pump seal going, until the fire covered it.

Haw sent everything at once.

*[Where? What? How many? Is it coming back? Can I—]*

Teasel answered her.

*[Two. Three? Small ~ counting, not sure of the count]*

Madder sent one line.

*[Let it go]*

Haw let it go, mostly.
