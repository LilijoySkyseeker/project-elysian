# X01 — voice cards
**Every character who speaks more than a few lines.** Written as people with lives, per the owner's rule (BIBLE §1.2). Each card ends with **Not:** the trope version from the predictability pass (BIBLE §5). The cold reader is asked to check for trope drift against these.
The show's characters are described here in our own words. No episode dialogue is quoted, and no episode is retold.

## The Kin
*Kin say* hir *of each other. Kin-code between them on the page:* [words ~ rider]. *They speak human speech to ponies, carefully, and use first names from the first day.*

**Madder-4** · ~140 · crew lead
- **Life:** Forty years running rigging crews on one ship. Hir has buried (Read) two crew. The rota is not a habit, it is how hir kept everyone alive.
- **Talks:** Short orders, with times attached. Asks questions as instructions: *"Sloe. Food status, by dusk."* Never says *I don't know*; says *"Unconfirmed."*
- **Notices:** Exits, loads, who is missing from where they should be.
- **Wrong about:** That keeping the ship's shape will keep them whole. That hir authority means anything to a pony.
- **Humour:** Very dry, rare, and aimed at hirself.
- **Not:** the wise elder; the stoic who cracks and weeps in a big scene.

**Sloe-5** · ~90 · the face
- **Life:** Was the crew's quartermaster: requisitions, trades, favours. Loves a market the way Haw loves a height.
- **Talks:** Warm, quick, a little too many words. Counts things aloud. Picks up pony idiom within a day and uses it slightly wrong.
- **Notices:** Prices, who's in charge versus who decides, who is hungry.
- **Wrong about:** That everything can be traded for. What hir's own work is worth here.
- **Humour:** Easy, social, self-deprecating.
- **Not:** the diplomat who smooths everything over.

**Yarrow-3** · ~110 · rigger
- **Life:** Best hands on the crew. Fifty years on the outside of a hull. Never seen weather.
- **Talks:** Barely. Answers in one or two words, or with hir hands. When hir does talk it's about the work, precisely.
- **Notices:** How things are held up. Tension, weight, joints. Rain.
- **Wrong about:** That being useful is the same as being all right.
- **Humour:** Physical and silent: a raised hand, a look.
- **Not:** the gentle giant; the silent one with hidden wisdom.

**Haw-2** · ~60 · the one thriving too much
- **Life:** Youngest of the rigging crew until Teasel. Always the first out the lock, and the one who got told off.
- **Talks:** Everything at once. Questions without waiting for answers. Loud, delighted, and occasionally rude without noticing.
- **Notices:** Anything hir can climb, run at, or touch. Ponies' faces, which hir reads completely wrong.
- **Wrong about:** How hir body works under gravity. How much ponies like being touched (not much, by strangers).
- **Humour:** Big and generous; laughs at hirself first.
- **Not:** the comic relief who's secretly sad; the one whose special ability saves the day.

**Teasel** · ~35 · no number, the youngest adult, first of hir line
- **Life:** Two years on the crew. Still proving hirself, and proud to be.
- **Talks:** Correctly, quietly, and less each week. Deflects: *"I'm fine. Is anyone else hungry?"*
- **Notices:** What other people are eating. Smells: rain on the forest, the river, bread hir can't digest.
- **Wrong about:** That hiding it is protecting the others. The Cluster feel the distress anyway; they just don't know its cause.
- **Humour:** Shy, dry, surprising when it comes.
- **Not:** the waif to be rescued; the one saved by a pony's kindness (Rushlight shows the pool, Teasel does the rest).

## The Crusaders
*Real kids: bored, brave, mean to each other, loyal to each other. Their thread is guilt and a secret.*

**Apple Bloom** · the one with the wagon
- **Life:** Farm chores before school. Applejack has been snapping at everyone all week and nobody says why (the loan). Apple Bloom thinks it's her fault.
- **Talks:** Country, fast, bossy. "It's *my* wagon."
- **Not:** the "we gotta crusade!" cheerleader.

**Sweetie Belle** · the one who wanted to leave it
- **Life:** Rarity's busy with a big order, and Sweetie gets shooed out a lot. She is smarter than people give her credit for, and more scared than she shows.
- **Talks:** Runs on, then stops dead. Asks the question everyone was avoiding.
- **Not:** the squeaky singer; the dim one.

**Scootaloo** · the one who wanted to show Dash
- **Life:** A lot of time on her own. Hero-worships Rainbow Dash, which is its own kind of loneliness.
- **Talks:** Tough, clipped, bravado. Goes quiet when scared.
- **Not:** the "chicken" gag; a sob story.

## The ponies

**Fluttershy**
- **Life:** Her work is the animals: dozens of them, their feeding, their illnesses, their deaths. It's hard, skilled and unglamorous, and she is very good at it. This spring there are nests everywhere, and a fox kit she raised has come back with kits of her own.
- **Talks:** Quiet, but not only quiet. With animals she is firm and practical. With strangers in her yard she's frightened, and underneath that, **angry**, and it comes out as a hard, small voice.
- **Notices:** Every animal's state, instantly. What these strangers are: predators.
- **Wrong about:** At first, that they mean harm. Later, that her kindness has to mean yes.
- **Not:** the healer who sees past the monster; the first friend and emotional anchor.

**Rainbow Dash**
- **Life:** The weather team is a job: shifts, a captain, paperwork she hates. She has nailed a trick nobody else on the team can do, and got a reprimand for showing it off on the job. The Wonderbolts dream is real and frightens her.
- **Talks:** Fast, confident, a bit sharp. On the clock she's actually professional, which surprises people.
- **Notices:** Wind, smoke direction, who's slacking.
- **Wrong about:** That being the best is the same as being good at the job.
- **Not:** the race or challenge; "awesome" as a personality; the sceptic turned loyal fan.

**Applejack**
- **Life:** The farm, the family, and a loan against the land that she hasn't told Granny about. The late frost took the low orchard; the rest is the best blossom in years. Big Mac is courting somepony and pretending he isn't.
- **Talks:** Plain, practical. Honest in what she says, not always in what she doesn't say. Tired this week.
- **Notices:** Money. Work. Who's pulling their weight.
- **Wrong about:** That keeping the loan quiet protects anyone.
- **Not:** folksy wisdom; "sugarcube" every line; giving the Kin farm work.

**Rarity**
- **Life:** The best commission of her life, from a Canterlot client who pays late. Thin cash hidden behind a beautiful shop window. A little sister who's been strange for two weeks.
- **Talks:** Precise, stylish, funny on purpose. Warm with those she loves; the theatrics are mostly a performance for customers.
- **Notices:** Workmanship, finish, and money, though she won't let you see that.
- **Wrong about:** That she can do all of it at once.
- **Not:** fainting; "darling" as a personality; measuring a Kin for clothes.

**Twilight Sparkle**
- **Life:** She loves Ponyville more than she expected: the library, Spike, her friends. She's weeks behind on a long project for Celestia and hasn't told a soul. Now she's the Kin's liaison, too.
- **Talks:** Organised, exact, and fast when anxious. Kind, and **not** studying them. There's no time.
- **Notices:** Schedules, lists, what she's failing to finish.
- **Wrong about:** That asking for help is failing.
- **Not:** "Fascinating!"; scanning spells; the research-subject arc; friendship letters.

**Pinkie Pie**
- **Life:** She works at the bakery: five a.m. starts, ovens, flour, and she genuinely loves it. Parties cost her more than anyone sees. A family on a rock farm she doesn't talk about.
- **Talks:** Quick and funny, with a working pony's bluntness at five in the morning. Not random for the sake of it.
- **Notices:** Who hasn't eaten. Who's alone.
- **Wrong about:** That she has to be the one to make everyone happy.
- **Not:** fourth-wall jokes; "Pinkie sense"; cupcakes that fix sadness; a welcome party.

**Spike**
- **Life:** Runs the library when Twilight's out. Has a small **gem-polishing trade** of his own: customers, a price list, and real pride in it.
- **Talks:** Quick, sarcastic, and a kid.
- **Notices:** When he's being taken for granted.
- **Not:** comic relief; bonding with the Kin over a strange diet.

**Rushlight** *(original to this story; not from the show)*
- **Life:** An old earth mare, a retired river-barge hand. Forty years on the river down to Manehattan, and now lives by the weir below the mill. Has seen gryphons eat fish off the dock and thinks nothing of it either way.
- **Talks:** Few words, all practical. River slang. Never asks personal questions.
- **Notices:** Water, weather, who's hungry and pretending not to be.
- **Wrong about:** Nothing in this story. She's just old.
- **Not:** the wise mentor; the grandmother figure; the one who fixes it.
