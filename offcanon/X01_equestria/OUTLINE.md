# X01 — chapter outline (DRAFT for the owner)
**Eight chapters, about 3,000–3,500 words each, about 26k in all. Time covered: the arrival night to about four weeks on.**
Each chapter is written through DOC-00H, with its own brief, predictability pass, cold read, and the owner's read as the gate. Everything here is a proposal. The *beats* are what must happen; *how* is the drafter's.

**Rules for every chapter** (BIBLE §1): the problems exist regardless of the Kin; every life pulls both ways; the Kin fix nothing for anyone; no episode rehash; no line from the predictable list (BIBLE §5).
**Language** (proposed, closing BIBLE §6): the show's convention. They understand each other from the start, and nobody asks why. The gaps are in vocabulary and manners: no pony word for *rail*, *frame* or *hull*; no Kin idea of *cutie mark*, *bits* or *hoof-shake*.
**Kin-code on the page:** *[words ~ rider]* between Kin only. Ponies see ears and silence.

### 1. Apple Bloom: *the mirror*
**Span:** one afternoon into evening. **POV:** Apple Bloom, close third.
- The three of them in the ruined castle on a dare that got out of hand. The mirror is heavy, cracked, and far too interesting. They drag it home on Apple Bloom's wagon through the Everfree at dusk, bickering: Scootaloo wants to show it to Rainbow Dash, Sweetie Belle wants to leave it, Apple Bloom insists it's hers because it's on her wagon.
- It wakes. For a few seconds it shows other places, fast and wrong, one after another. Then something bright and *regulated* holds, and the mirror comes apart.
- Heat. Things on fire. A heap of shapes where there was nothing: big, dark, too many legs, moving.
- **They run.** The wagon is left behind.
- **Threads:** the Crusaders (↓ they caused it); Apple Bloom's own life (↑ a good day with her friends until now; ↓ Applejack has been short-tempered all week and Apple Bloom doesn't know why, which is the loan).
- **Ending: cut mid-action.** Running. **Escalates:** yes.
- **Not:** a cutie-mark scheme; comedy panic.

### 2. Madder-4: *theta*
**Span:** the same night, to near dawn. **POV:** Madder-4, close third. *(The one who refuses: procedure as a handrail.)*
- They wake out of theta lock in a heap, under gravity. Void-built bodies fold. Madder counts presences: five, all there, whole. Then hir reaches for the ship and there is nothing in the sky. Within a minute every one of them has worked out what the ship felt: five presences stopping in one tenth of a second. Nobody says it. Madder gives orders instead.
- Scrap from the reactor, plasma burning in the undergrowth. Not panic, just *let's not be here*. Madder sets a watch rota. Nobody has a watch to keep.
- The forest: smell, noise, wet. **The first animal any of them has ever seen** is something ordinary (a moth at the fire, an owl), and it frightens a sixty-kilo rigger more than the fire does.
- The walk out, on bodies that don't work under gravity. Haw is enjoying it, which Madder cannot forgive.
- **Threads:** Madder (↑ they're alive and hir rota keeps them moving; ↓ home thinks hir let four people die with hir); Haw (↑↑).
- **Ending: image, no comment.** A small house at the forest's edge, and it is full of animals. **Escalates:** yes (the realisation about home comes after the first relief).
- **Not:** a panic scene over the silent band; stars; pointing home.

### 3. Fluttershy: *the yard*
**Span:** dawn to the afternoon of day one. **POV:** Fluttershy, close third.
- Her animals panic before she's awake: five predators in the yard. The chickens, the rabbits, the birds. One of the rabbits bolts into the forest toward the smoke and doesn't come back. She is frightened and **angry**, and she has a right to be.
- She doesn't let them in. They sit in her yard all morning, not knowing that's worse. She has animals to feed anyway, and she feeds them around the strangers, furious, because the work doesn't stop.
- The Kin, who have never met an animal, are fascinated and useless. One of them (Haw) tries to help and makes it worse.
- **Rainbow Dash** comes at noon *on weather-team business*: there's a fire at the forest edge, the team's being called, keep the animals in. She finds five of *them* in Fluttershy's yard. Dash is on the clock and on a reprimand, and she handles it like a pegasus with a job: brusque, protective, not impressed and not hostile.
- **Threads:** Fluttershy (↑ spring, the fox kits, her pride in her work; ↓ the lost rabbit and the panic); Dash (↑ the trick she's nailed; ↓ the reprimand, and now this on top).
- **Ending: a line of dialogue.** Fluttershy's, and not a kind one. **Escalates:** yes.
- **Not:** Fluttershy seeing past the monster; Dash's challenge or race; "awesome".

### 4. Sloe-5: *the market*
**Span:** days two to four. **POV:** Sloe-5, close third. *(Adapts, and pays for it.)*
- The Crusaders' story has spread: garbled, monstrous, and half-believed. The Mayor, a lot of closed doors, and one empty building (an old barn or a disused mill) given to them because it's far from anything.
- **Food.** Five of them need about 10,000 kcal of protein a day. Ponyville eats flowers, hay and cake. Sloe learns the market: eggs, cheese, beans and nuts are what's usable, and there aren't enough of them. They have no bits. Sloe trades work, and is bad at knowing what work is worth here.
- **Applejack** sells to hir: commerce, not charity. The farm needs money and she needs it more than she'll say. She drives a fair bargain and a hard one. Sloe likes her enormously.
- **Pinkie** is at the bakery at five in the morning, on shift, flour to the elbows. There's a moment where a welcome party is plainly on her mind, and she doesn't throw one. (The reason is hers; the chapter doesn't explain it.)
- **Threads:** Sloe (↑ markets delight hir, and hir is good at people; ↓ always tired, and the arithmetic doesn't close); Applejack (↓ the loan; ↑ a buyer); Pinkie (↑ the bakery; ↓ the private cost).
- **Ending: document end.** A tab, in Applejack's handwriting, and the running total. **Escalates:** the numbers do.
- **Not:** a welcome party; farm work for the Kin; charity.

### 5. Twilight: *liaison*
**Span:** the first week, over several nights. **POV:** Twilight, close third.
- Celestia has asked her to "look after the visitors". She is already weeks behind on the project she owes Celestia, and hasn't told anyone. The Kin are one more thing, and she is *good* at it, conscientious and kind and stretched. She is not studying them; there is no time.
- **Spike** is running the library while she's out. He has something of his own going on (in the outline: a correspondence he is proud of, or a small business in gem-polishing). Neither of them notices the other is struggling.
- Twilight visits the barn late one night with a question that could have waited, and sees the Cluster asleep: five bodies in one heap, not moving, not breathing that she can see. For a long terrible moment she thinks they're dead. They aren't. She leaves without waking them, and doesn't tell anyone what she thought.
- **Threads:** Twilight (↑ she loves this town, the library, her friends; ↓ the hidden project, and now this); Spike (↑ his thing; ↓ being small).
- **Ending: unresolved.** Her unfinished project, open on the desk, and the night half gone. **Escalates:** weakly, and that's deliberate. This is the quiet chapter.
- **Not:** scanning spells; "fascinating"; a friendship letter.

### 6. Yarrow-3: *rain*
**Span:** week two. **POV:** Yarrow-3, close third. *(Wants to work.)*
- The weather team is soaking the forest edge. The Everfree's weather runs itself, so they can only work the border, and they argue about whether it's even their job. The rain falls on Ponyville's side too. It is **Yarrow's first rain, ever.** Hir can't stop going out in it.
- A half-job: hauling, anchoring, the kind of work where hir hands are good and hir body is wrong for the gravity. Dash is on the crew. They work well together for about an hour, until Dash's showing-off costs her with the team captain in front of everyone, and Yarrow sees it happen and has no idea what hir's seeing.
- **Haw** has been climbing everything in town: roofs, the clock tower, trees. It's *wonderful*. Void-built bodies misjudge falls under gravity.
- **Threads:** Yarrow (↑ rain, work; ↓ half a job is not a job); Dash (↓ the reprimand made public); Haw (↑↑, about to be ↓).
- **Ending: cut mid-action.** Haw, at a height, and the sound. **Escalates:** yes.
- **Not:** a Kin skill that saves the weather team; a rescue.

### 7. Rarity: *the commission*
**Span:** one night and the next day, in week two. **POV:** Rarity, close third.
- Rarity is three days from delivering the best work of her life to a Canterlot client who hasn't paid for the last one. She is thrilled and frightened and doesn't let either show.
- **Haw is badly hurt:** a forelimb, broken and worse. (Gore is allowed; keep it plain.) Ponyville's hospital does what it can for a body it doesn't understand. The Kin say *six years* to regrow a limb, and no pony can take that in.
- **Sweetie Belle** has been strange for two weeks. Tonight, after hearing about Haw, she tells Rarity everything: the castle, the mirror, the running. *It's our fault.*
- Rarity has to decide what to do with that, on the night her commission is due. She does something imperfect.
- **Threads:** Rarity (↑ the commission; ↓ the client, the cash, her sister); the Crusaders (↓↓); Haw (↓↓).
- **Ending: reversal.** Whatever Rarity decided comes out differently than she meant. **Escalates:** yes. This is the story's worst week.
- **Not:** Rarity measuring a Kin for clothes; fainting; "darling" as a personality.

### 8. Teasel: *what there is*
**Span:** weeks three and four. **POV:** Teasel, close third. *(Quietly failing.)*
- Teasel can't eat what there is and is losing weight. The Cluster can feel that hir is in distress, but hir won't tell them why. The **fish** in the river are the obvious protein, and to ponies eating an animal is monstrous. Fluttershy lives two hundred metres downstream.
- The one pony who is kind to Teasel without fuss (to be chosen: not a main-six trope role; possibly Big Macintosh, or a minor pony).
- The Crusaders come to the barn, sent or not sent (Rarity's decision from ch. 7), to say it to the Kin themselves. The five do not agree about what to say back. Madder is cold, Haw (arm bound) laughs, Sloe tries to manage it, Yarrow says nothing, and Teasel…
- Life goes on at four weeks: a tab at the Apple stall, rain some days, a rota nobody keeps.
- **Threads:** Teasel (↓ hunger; ↑ the one kind pony, and the smell of the forest after rain); the Crusaders (↓ the telling; ↑ it's told); everyone else, glimpsed.
- **Ending: image, no comment** (or unresolved). **No** group hug, lesson, letter or stars. **Escalates:** the hunger does.
- **Not:** a fix for Teasel's hunger by a pony; forgiveness as resolution.
