# WARGAME 03 — The Kin Against Themselves: The Species as a Story Engine
**Codename:** Project Elysian | **Species:** Aethela | **Self-Name:** The Kin
**Date:** 2026-09-22 · **Against:** CANON v2.9 (D-1…D-90), DOC-00…DOC-13, scenes S001–S016
**Classification:** Wargame record. Proposes nothing as canon; findings N-01…N-12 and the H-register await ruling, as G-items did.
**Lineage:** W01 = seven outside-context wargames (→ D-70…D-72); W02 = internal wargame rounds 1–2 (→ D-76…D-90). This one turns the species on itself.

---

### 0. THE FRAME

Every earlier wargame asked *does the mechanism hold?* This one asks the question the archive exists to answer and has never audited: **do the Kin do their job?** The job is to be a source of stories worth telling, under three constraints the archive already enforces — biologically plausible, logically sound, self-consistent. A species can pass every physics audit and still be a bad protagonist. A species can be a wonderful protagonist and be a cheat.

Method. (1) Inventory the **engines** — the mechanisms that generate conflict without a writer's thumb on the scale — and name the canon each rests on. (2) Inventory the **foreclosures** — what the design makes impossible — and sort them into *costs paid on purpose* and *leaks*. (3) **Load-test**: run fourteen ordinary story premises against canon and record what survives, what survives changed, what dies. (4) Re-run the **Mary-Sue guard** (D-12, D-19) as a ledger: every superiority against the cost that *reaches the page*. (5) The **temptation map**: the lines a scene will want to cross, and whether canon answers with a wall, a price, or silence. (6) Findings.

A note on scale, because pacing is a story property. 45,000 Kin in Sol at 4–12 a Cluster is ~5,600 Clusters. D-78's 320 births a year is one child per Cluster every ~17 years; D-35's hazard is ~110 deaths a year — **a Reading somewhere in Sol every three days, and in any one Cluster about every forty-five years.** Rare enough that each is an event; common enough that every adult has knelt at one. The demographics are already tuned for fiction, which is worth saying out loud because it was not the reason they were chosen.

### 2. THE FORECLOSURES — what the design closes

The Kin are a species defined by subtraction: no sex, no romance, no aging, no disease, no possessions, no money that matters, no writing, no war, no crime of property, no gods, no anonymity, no lying about state. Each subtraction closes a genre. The question is whether each closure was *paid for* (a cost the design acknowledges and converts) or is a *leak* (a genre gone with nothing gained).

| Closed | Closure | Paid? | What replaces it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Romance | D-65 | **Paid.** The assuming is the story; the one-in-ten-thousand Hum is intensity without category. | The human who keeps assuming; a Kin who wants a category and has none. |
| War / combat | D-79 | **Paid.** *Dioscuri* — a war with no blow — is better than a war. | Rescue, disaster, the burn, MACTAC as competence. |
| Heist / theft | D-60, D-83 | **Paid, narrowly.** Nothing to steal but Core tissue, a body before it is burned, a Genesis Module, a name. | The biotech thriller: an operator's lab, tissue theft, the re-derivation hole. |
| Whodunnit | D-85 (every death to the second on the Hum) | **Half-paid.** *Who/when/where* is free; only *why* and *at-request-or-not* survive — see N-05. | The Reading-denied; the vetoed crime; the thin day. |
| Impostor / secret identity | no anonymity among Kin | **Paid** against Kin; open against humans (a human with no console). | The Kin on Earth. |
| Amnesia | D-13 (Core loss = death) | **Paid.** Data Rot and the veto are better amnesias. | The old who feel it only in dreams (D-82). |
| Plague / medical drama | D-36 | **Leak, acknowledged.** "Kin have little motivation to pursue medicine" closes a genre and gains only regeneration-as-surgery. Acceptable; note it. | Prosthetics; the six-year arm. |
| Religion / theodicy | D-64 | **Paid.** *Carried / lost* is a complete sacred vocabulary; the Written are the saints. | The Weave as liturgy on arks. |
| Coming into money / class | D-60 | **Paid.** Memory is the currency (D-83); the grant-web is the class system. | Old money = a high count. |
| Villainy by violence | D-66, D-79 | **Paid — but under-stocked.** See N-11: the Kin villain is a *curator*, and the roles exist without names. | The Weaver who chose wrong; the re-feeler who forged; the parent who tuned. |
| Political power *among* Kin | no polity; wards of the Compact | **Leak, partial.** The Weave is the only institution and its sanctions are unruled — see N-03. | The sanction of not being carried. |

**Verdict.** Nine closures paid, two leaks (medicine, intra-Kin politics), one half-paid (the mystery). The design pays its debts. The two leaks are addressed by N-03 and N-11 below without new biology.

### 8. THE SHAPE OF THE ANSWER

**Do the Kin do their job?** Yes — and for the right reason. The species' story value lives almost entirely in its costs: the un-Readable human, the seventeen minutes, the weather everyone hears, the memory that keeps facts and loses meaning. Fourteen engines, none needing a cheat; twelve of fourteen ordinary premises survive contact with canon; eleven of the walls a writer will hit are walls that generate tension rather than delete a genre; the Mary-Sue ledger has one unpaid line. The design pays its debts on the page, which is a stricter test than paying them in a table, and it passes.

**Where it is weakest** is not in any mechanism but in three silences and one habit. The silences: what a Kin can do *against* the ranking (N-02), whether the species ever imposes its one damnation (N-03), and what a Kin alone is (N-04). The habit: the audit loop's instinct to number every silence, which has been the archive's virtue for four days and would, left running, sand off the hooks that make the rest worth reading (N-10). The H-register is the fix for the habit; the three rulings are one sentence each.
