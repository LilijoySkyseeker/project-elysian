# SCENE 004 — The Dual-Spectrum Recital
**Origin:** DOC-05 Scene 04 (v1.2) · **Theme:** Aesthetic alignment · **POV:** Mixed (Chief Eng. Miller / Lyra-4)
**Style:** DOC-00 governs names and pronouns (human POV says *she*; Kin POV and documentary voice say *hir*).  
**Canon status:** ORIGINAL TEXT PRESERVED — drift notes below; not yet revised.

---

The rec room on Habitat Station *Artemis-9* was packed. Human crew members sat along the tiered seating, while several Aethela sat curled together on the padded lower carpet, their long tails loosely intertwined.

At the center of the floor stood Lyra-4. Hir vulpine-taur frame was immaculate, hir dense chestnut fur brushed until it gleamed under the soft warm spotlights. In hir upper humanoid hands, she held an ancient, beautifully restored wooden cello.

A hush fell over the room. Lyra drew the bow across the C-string. 

The deep, resonant wooden voice of the cello filled the room—a Bach suite. But as Lyra played the physical instrument, she tilted hir head back, hir swiveling ears flaring slightly. From hir throat spilled a delicate, crystalline vocal harmony—two soaring acoustic whistle-tones that floated perfectly above the cello's dark bassline.

Beside the stage hung a massive two-meter canvas Lyra had painted the previous week. To the human crew, it was a swirling, moody landscape of Jupiter's Great Red Spot. But as the music reached its climax, Lyra emitted a soft, invisible bio-RADAR pulse from hir ears. 

The pulse bounced off the raised, metallic-pigment strokes of the canvas, scattering a subtle, phase-shifted echo back into the room. For the Aethela sitting on the floor, the room seemed to bloom with an invisible, three-dimensional geometry that danced in perfect synchronization with the cello's counterpoint.

In the audience, Chief Engineer Miller closed his eyes, a warm smile spreading across his face. 

"Damn, Lyra," he whispered when the last note faded into the airlock vents. "Your creators really knew what they were doing."

Lyra set the cello down gently, bowed hir upper torso, and gave a cheerful, warbling acoustic whistle—a sound of pure, unadulterated artistic pride.

*Over the private RF mesh, she sent a tiny, warm pulse to hir cluster-mates:* **[Data Packet: Pure Joy / Human-Audience Resonance: 98.4%]**.
