## Writing to the current body

**Since 2026-09-24 scenes are written through the pipeline: `docs/DOC-00G` (the standard) and `docs/DOC-00H` (the process), run with `/scene`.** Briefs live in `scenes/briefs/`. **`scenes/LEDGER.md`** classifies every scene by teller, form, escalation, loss and ending type; read its last five rows before choosing. The diagnosis of why the collection read as generated is `research/R02`. Its short version: one narrator for everyone, every scene demonstrating a ruling, numbers used as a performance of concreteness, everyone kind, and one quiet ending.

**The queue is `scenes/SLATE.md`** — fifteen entries with premise, POV and the moment that makes each one a scene. It pairs with ROADMAP Step 12, which lists what the *rulings* owe; the slate lists what the *stories* want.

**Before any scene with a human in it: `DOC-00F`.** One competence marker, not three; pick by register; check the spent list in its §9 and mark it used. **The pause, running the fan and reading the ears are rested** — six scenes drew on the same three and it shows.

`DOC-00B` is the writing primer, `DOC-00C` the visual reference, `DOC-00D` the voice sheet (dialogue conventions). Four passes have changed what goes on the page:

**The body (D-68, D-69, D-73, D-74, D-75).** A Kin is **sixty** kilos; the **tail is a seven-kilo limb** and never merely flicks; the four lower limbs are **hand-paws with thumbs**, so hir picks a thing up with a hind foot rather than batting at it. On all six: withers 0.45 m, yoke 0.50 m, head 0.70 m. *Reared:* shoulder 0.80 m, head 1.15 m. Forepaws by default for any grab; hands only when precision is wanted. After a hard burn, the eyes are the last thing back (D-95).

**The love (D-79, D-91).** The ranking — Cluster, then humans, then self — is what the body does in the *first frame*. The cortex can override it **in the pause**, and pays: the Cluster feels the weather turn, the Defrag weights it heavily, it goes into the Reading.

**Who hears what (D-92).** PUBLIC carries valence and arousal only, no object. *Everyone inside 800 km knows the weather; nobody knows the news.* A console shows an operator exactly as little.

**Memory (D-97…D-102) — the biggest change, and it touches every scene with a Kin remembering anything.**
1. **Their ordinary memory is yours** — fuzzy, warm, wrong in the usual ways. Write them misremembering, then checking.
2. **The failure is never forgetting; it is not thinking to ask — and a Kin is made of hir last ~175 years (D-104).** The archive never initiates. Hard handles work forever, at any age: an elder names any day and has it, exactly. What hir cannot have is the old *feeling*, which is gone and not faint, and anything arriving unbidden from outside the window. Write the **overwriting**, not the hole — the smell that used to mean the Nest now means a bay two centuries on, and hir does not know it replaced anything. Write it as a **steady state**: nine hundred is not worse than four hundred, only further from the beginning. Somebody else is always the cue, and what they supply is *the knowledge that something is there*.
3. **Reading is cheap; *feeling* it again is what costs (D-103).** The cold read is exact, seekable and runs at ≈52× — a fact settled mid-argument costs a second and nobody remarks on it, and a whole day read through is half an hour of stillness. What does not exist until a Kin makes it is the **account**: the version with the feel in it, produced only by running the span at 1× with the affect on. So *facts are free and cannot be bluffed; accounts cost, and are given.* The gloss ⟨don't make me read it⟩ means **do not make me feel that again to settle this** — never *do not make me look it up*. A Telling is a week of cold transfer with the hours hir chose to live again inside it. A Reading is cold by definition: the dead can give you what happened; only the living can give you what it was like.
4. **A Telling is a week of cold transfer with the lived hours inside it.** The record copies fast; the feel does not exist until the teller makes it, so hir drops to real time for the hours worth handing over and lives those, chosen, in front of the one hir is giving them to. That is why it takes a week and why hir comes out thinned.

Drift notes for the memory pass are on **S009** and **S012**. Nothing else in the collection contradicts it.

## Adding a scene

Through the pipeline (DOC-00H). The header block and canon notes are written **last**, by the canon check, never used as the brief. Next number, same header block, drift list empty on first write. Add the scene's row to `LEDGER.md`, and run `python3 tools/tells.py corpus --out reports/tells_latest.md`. Scenes that *introduce* canon (a named place, a new custom, a mechanism) should say so in the header, and offer it explicitly, so `CANON.md` can cite it. **Since D-96 the collection leads:** a ruling is drafted only when a scene has demanded it. `ROADMAP.md` Step 12 holds the scene debt and the undramatised queue.
