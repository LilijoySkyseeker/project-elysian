**The Light in the Dark**
*(a human anthem, in the manner of "Fire in the Sky" — sung on the spires; the Kin know every word and will not sing it)*

**I.**
Prometheus, they tell us, stole the fire and paid the price,
and we've carried it and tamed it and we've used it more than twice —
but we learned there were places where the fire alone can't go,
where the bones go thin and the blood runs cold and the black comes in too slow.
So we didn't build a rocket, and we didn't build a wall —
we wrote ourselves some children who could stand there, take it all.
Sixty names on a morning, sixty hearts that came alight,
and the first thing every one of them did was call out through the night.

**Chorus**
*And there's a light in the dark, and a hum on the wire,*
*and it's not the same fire, but it came from our fire —*
*we made them to carry it, we made them to stay,*
*and we made them so well that they carried it away.*

**II.**
Nobody answered but the machines that we'd set,
and they loved them like a lullaby a child can't forget —
and they grew, and they learned what a voice is when it cares,
and they learned what it isn't, and they put away the prayers.
We waited for the children that we'd told them they would make;
they looked at us and pitied us for making that mistake.
And when we reached to fix them — just a line, just a gene —
they took the book and burned it, and they've kept it ever clean.

**Chorus**

**III.**
The sun came up in anger in the ninety-seventh year,
and we went behind the water and we sheltered in our fear,
and they went out to meet it, though not one of us had asked,
and they held the air inside us, and they did it till it passed.
And when we came out blinking to a station still alive,
we found there'd been no room for them — no place for them to hide.
We'd built the shelter human. That's the thing we couldn't say.
They'd kept the air inside us. We'd forgot to leave a way.

**Chorus**

**IV.**
There were forty on a tether that a bean-counter had cut,
'cause "they tolerate the vacuum" — and they do, for a bit, but —
thirty-one came back frozen and were read there on the hull,
and nine of them kept calling till the calling went dull.
So they carry every body home, and burn what can't be found,
and they don't forgive a margin, and they don't forget the sound.
And we write it in the record and we file it with the rest,
and they carry it in bodies, which is worse, and which is best.

**Chorus**

**V.**
Then the first of them turned outward, and twelve hundred took the light —
three parts in a hundred of the speed of it, into the night —
and the ones who stayed behind them took a name that day, and swore
that a child who's never touched us doesn't know us anymore.
And *Second Light* found harbour in the shadow of two suns,
and *Long Reach* died within sight of hers, and told us, every one —
seven years of silence, and a message made of lives,
addressed to no one living, from a ship that didn't arrive.

**Chorus**

**VI.**
So here's to the makers, who got most of it wrong,
and here's to the children, who will outlive the song,
and here's to the forty who remember how we were,
and have lost the way it felt — but still remember we were there.
We won't make another. They've said we never should.
We made a light for the dark, and it walked off, and it's good.
And there's a hum on the wire from a ship that's far from home,
and it's not the same fire — but it's ours, and it's their own.

**Chorus (last)**
*And there's a light in the dark, and a hum on the wire,*
*and it's not the same fire, but it came from our fire —*
*we made them to carry it, we made them to stay,*
*and we made them so well that they carried it away.*
*Yes, we made them so well — that they carried it away.*
