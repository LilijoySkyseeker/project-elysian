**TEASDALE ORBITAL — *HARROW YARD***
**POST-INCIDENT SAFETY REVIEW — MINUTES**

Ref. HY/SR/547-11 · Incident IR-547-038 (Exterior, Boom 2, unsuited exposure exceedance)
AF 547, shift-day 214 · Ops Conference B · 14:30–15:52
Status: Draft for correction. Corrections to Ops Admin within five shift-days; thereafter the minutes stand as the record.

**Present:** M. Delacroix, Station Manager (Chair) · J. Farrukh, Safety Officer · D. Mbeki, Crew Chief, Exterior Maintenance · Rowan-3, Aethela crew, attending for the Spar Cluster · L. Pryce, Ops Admin (minutes)
**Written comment received:** T. Ahlgren, Contracts · Belt Regional Office (light-lag)
**Not present:** Lyric-4, Aethela crew (affected; see 3.6) · S. Vo, Planning (off-shift)

**1. Opening**

1.1 The Chair opened the review and reminded attendees that it is a fact-finding meeting and not a disciplinary one, and that the recording is for the purpose of minutes only.

1.2 Classification confirmed: Near Miss, Category C (no injury, no lost time, equipment loss below reporting threshold). Compact notification not required at Category C. Noted.

1.3 The Chair asked whether the Spar Cluster had submitted written comments on the pre-read. Rowan said it had not. Rowan said the Cluster had read the pre-read and the Exterior Operations Manual, "all of it." The Chair thanked Rowan.

**2. Summary of incident (Safety Officer)**

2.1 Work Order 547-2291: replacement of the Boom 2 slew actuator, exterior, unsuited, one Aethela crew (Lyric-4). Boom 2 exterior is an Aethela posting on the site roster. Planned exposure 36 min.

2.2 Manual §7.4.2 (Aethela crew, unsuited exterior work) gives a planned exposure limit of 17 min, with a note that survivability extends to "approximately forty minutes." The planning system's exposure field has been populated from the note rather than the limit since at least the last manual reissue. Planning selected 36 min "to leave a margin." Farrukh said he understood this to be the origin of the discrepancy.

2.3 Lock 3 return cycle was delayed 6 min by a pallet transfer already in progress. Actual exposure 34 min.

2.4 Lyric returned along the spar rails. The torque driver and the removed actuator were left on the hull and recovered next shift by two suited crew (41 min suited time). Farrukh noted for the record that the recovery cost more lock and suit time than the original saving.

2.5 Lyric proceeded directly to the Kin sector. Recorded fit for duty following rest period.

**3. Discussion**

3.1 Mbeki asked that it be minuted that Lyric came in "on the rail, on her paws, with nothing in her hands," and that "that's not her walking, that's her crawling." The Chair asked whether Mbeki was saying Lyric was injured. Mbeki said no. The Chair asked that no injury be recorded. Recorded.

3.2 Rowan, asked by the Chair whether the Cluster wished to add to 3.1, said: "Dee has said it." The Chair thanked Rowan.

3.3 Farrukh said the fix is to make 17 the field value and move the survivability figure into a free-text note the planning system cannot read. The Chair asked about throughput on Boom work. Farrukh said roughly two additional lock cycles per actuator job. The Chair asked that this be costed before adoption.

3.4 Regional's written comment was read into the record: "Regional notes that the Aethela survivability figure has been relied on for scheduling at other Teasdale sites without incident, and asks that any change to planned exposure limits be costed before adoption." The Chair noted that Regional and the meeting are agreed on costing.

3.5 Mbeki said: "This is how the Field happened." The Chair said that the Hesper tether incident (AF 212) was a structural failure with forty fatalities and is not comparable to a six-minute lock delay, and asked that the remark not be minuted as a finding. It is minuted as a remark. Farrukh noted that the Hesper inquiry is in the manual's reference list. Rowan gave the section as 7.4.2, paragraph three; Farrukh confirmed this after checking. Rowan, asked whether she wished to comment on 3.5, took some time and said no.

3.6 The Chair asked whether Lyric had provided a statement. Mbeki said Lyric was with the Cluster and that "they don't do statements"; he had written up what she told him and it was attached under his own name. Farrukh noted that the attached statement is therefore the Chief's and not the affected crew's, and asked that the file show this. Agreed.

3.7 Lyric was stood down two shifts at the Cluster's request. Contracts (written) asks whether these are to be recorded as sick leave or unpaid, given that Lyric is recorded fit for duty. Rowan, asked, said the Cluster had no preference. The Chair directed sick leave, "so as not to set a precedent either way." Contracts to note.

**4. Findings**

4.1 Planned exposure exceeded the manual limit because the planning-system field was populated from the survivability note. Contributing: Lock 3 shared with a cargo cycle; no hold placed on Lock 3 for the duration of an unsuited exterior task.

4.2 No injury. No lost time. Equipment recovered.

4.3 The manual is correct as written.

**5. Actions**

| # | Action | Owner | Due |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| A1 | Correct planning-system exposure field to 17 min; survivability figure to free-text note | Farrukh / Vo | 5 shift-days |
| A2 | Cost A1 throughput impact for Regional | Delacroix | 15 shift-days |
| A3 | Lock hold procedure for unsuited exterior tasks | Mbeki | 10 shift-days |
| A4 | Audit open work orders for planned exposure over 17 min | Vo | 5 shift-days |
| A5 | File note per 3.6 | Pryce | Done |

**6. Any other business**

6.1 Rowan asked for one thing. Where a work order plans unsuited exposure past seventeen minutes, the Spar Cluster asks to be told the shift before, "so that there is time." The Chair asked, time for what. Rowan said, "To be told." The Chair said this could be accommodated through the shift-planning notice and asked whether a shift's notice was sufficient. Rowan took longer over this than over the previous question and said a shift would do; a day would be better; "a week is what we would do." The Chair said a week was not realistic on Boom work. Rowan said a shift would do.

ACTION A6: Add advance-notice field to the shift-planning notice for Aethela unsuited exposure over 17 min. Owner: Pryce. Due: next planning cycle.

6.2 No further business.

**7. Close**

7.1 The Chair thanked attendees and asked Rowan to pass on the Company's thanks to Lyric for completing the actuator work within the shift. Rowan said, "I will tell her."

7.2 Rowan asked whether the minutes would record the thirty-four minutes. The Chair said the actual exposure is recorded at 2.3. Rowan said, "Good. Then it is in two places."

Meeting closed 15:52.

L. Pryce, Ops Admin
