## 1. The want
Quill-2 wants to keep a crock of something fermenting in the shared galley, where it has been for sixty-three years, and be left alone about it.

## 2. The obstacle / what goes wrong after the middle
- **Obstacle:** a retired human on the deck, D. Farrow, has filed a complaint about the smell, and the only way to answer a complaint on this station is **in writing, on the ticket**. The Kin do not write. When Quill asks to come and talk to Facilities instead, the answer is *needs to be on the ticket, sorry*.
- **After the middle:** it gets *settled*. A lid, a seal and a labelled shelf are agreed, and Facilities marks the ticket resolved. Then Farrow, not satisfied, files a separate report under a different category ("unlabelled biological material, shared food prep area"). That report routes to Safety, not Facilities. The coordinator who knew the history is on leave, and the relief coordinator removes the crock on schedule, by the book, to the recycler. Nobody who agreed anything was asked.

## 3. What is lost
The ferment: sixty-three years of a living culture. Whether any of it survives is the open question at the end. Quill asks whether the recycler intake has been flushed yet, because a scraping would be enough to start it again. **The thread ends before anyone answers.**

## 5. Shape
- **Form:** a facilities ticket thread, entries in time order, each with a header line (sender, date and time, status). No narration. A second ticket number appears when Farrow files the hazard report, and the thread shows the cross-reference.
- **Tense & person:** whatever each writer uses. Mostly present and first.
- **Target length:** about 1,400 words. The collection has been 1,400–2,200; this sits at the short end, and the form is quick to read.
- **Ending type:** **unresolved.** The last entry is Quill's short question about the recycler intake, followed by the system line *Status: Open — awaiting response*. Nothing after.

## 6. Lore budget (DOC-00G §4)
- **Mechanic 1: for a Kin, writing is a loss (D-101).** Quill has to argue for the crock in the medium her species will not use for anything that matters, and her messages are correct, short and inadequate. Each leaks one line. **Never explained.** The reader sees only that she will not write more than she must, and asks to talk instead.
- **Mechanic 2: the literate nose; food as composition (DOC-13 §4).** Farrow describes the smell as rot. Quill, asked to describe it, gives a **recipe**: exact, dry, grams and degrees. It is the least translatable answer possible, because a human reads a recipe as ingredients and a Kin reads it as the whole composition. She never reaches for poetic *notes* or *chords* (predicted, §10).
- **Texture, allowed, never explained:** the seam between the human and Kin sectors; the Nest; Kin names with numbers; AF dates in the ticket headers; the extractor refit.
- **Absent on purpose:** the pause, the Core, the ears, the tail, the fan (it is a galley, but the *fan* beat is rested: the extractor refit is the equipment, not the kindness), seventeen minutes, any rite, any Kin-code, any quoting of someone's words back at them (T16, rested). **No "eleven" and no "four" as a count** (the ticket number 4471 is fine: a label, not a count).
