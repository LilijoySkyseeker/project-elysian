# BRIEF — S026 *Guest Slot* (working title)
**Slate entry:** none. A new premise, written for the DOC-00H §15 cold-session test (*"a short standalone story about a Cluster"*). · **Ledger gap it fills:** a scene with **no human in it at all** (wanted since S024), and a tone that is **not "comic, then sad"** (S020, S024, S025 in a row): this one is comic, then raw, then a little better. It does not fill the length gap (target ~2,800) or second person.
**Status:** brief → predicted → drafted → linted → cold-read → revised → owner → canon → filed

## 1. The want
Fescue has had four broken nights in a row, wants one whole night's sleep, and the stranger who has been billeted in hir bed won't lie still.

## 2. The obstacle / what goes wrong after the middle
- **Obstacle:** the guest, Aster, is restless. Hir tail keeps doing the wrong pattern when the heap settles, so the settle has to be done again, and all night hir is awake and wound tight, which keeps waking Fescue beside hir. Fescue gets pettier by the hour, and it leaks.
- **At the middle:** at ten past three Aster's own Cluster, 340 km away, has its child, early. Every Kin in range hears the child arrive and answers it in the same second, the host heap and Aster included, but only the ones in the room with the child can give it a name. Fescue has already sent Aster something mean, exact, a minute before, and it cannot be taken back.
- **After the middle (the setback the first half does not predict):** a quarter of an hour later the whole Cluster on *Oxley Ring* goes quiet at once, the way Kin go quiet when something is wrong, and the child's heartbeat, which every one of them can hear on the long-range channel, goes thin and irregular. From 340 km they get the weather and never the news. Six Kin lie awake in a hammock listening to a stranger's newborn struggle, and nobody can do anything about it. **Nobody fixes this. There is no radio call** (see §10, version 1).

## 3. What is lost
- **Aster** loses the child's first minute. Hir answers hir own Cluster's child from 340 km, exactly as every stranger in range does, and is not in the frame when it is named. That does not come again.
- **Fescue** loses the night, and something worse: hir mean line is on the record, and all six of them had it, the guest included, at the worst moment of the guest's year. Nobody mentions it afterwards, and that doesn't take it back.
- **Rime** is wrong about the quiet on *Oxley Ring*. When a whole Cluster goes quiet at once, Kin read it as a death, and Rime says so to Aster, clean, with an elder's authority, while the child's heartbeat is still there to be heard, faint and ragged. It is Fescue, the tech, who says the carrier is still up. Rime does not take it back, and goes to sleep.

- **Mallow**, ~120, the face: warm, talks too much, leaks everything, heckles. Winning the bet about the relay.

Hir own Cluster is on *Oxley Ring*, 340 km sunward: too far for the frame, near enough to feel their weather. Polite, formal, stiff, and awake. **Not** the saintly sufferer. Hir is short with Bryony, and hir tail is rude without meaning to be.

## 5. Shape
- **Form:** short story.
- **Tense & person:** past, close third. (The collection's default, and X01's. Chosen for the exemplar, not for rotation; say so in the ledger.)
- **Target length:** ~2,800 words.
- **Ending type:** **image, no comment.** It is not in the last five rows (quiet withheld truth, coda, coda, cut mid-action, unresolved). The owner loves endings that are images or other people's lines, left unanswered.
- **Shape of the night:** (1) evening, the settle: comedy of the weave and the guest, Fescue's grievances, the Cluster heckling in code. (2) The small hours: Aster awake, Fescue awake because of it, the relay fault, the mean line. (3) Ten past three: the child, and the answer. (4) The quiet on *Oxley Ring*, and the heap listening to a heartbeat at 340 km for as long as it takes: talk in the dark, several threads, Rime wrong, Bryony asking, Sorb unable to lie still, and Fescue listening the only way hir knows, as a signal against the noise floor. When Rime reads the quiet as a death, Fescue is the one who says the child is still transmitting. It is not comfort; it is a tech contradicting an elder, and hir is short about it. That is the useful thing hir does, and all it does is keep them listening. (5) The heartbeat steadies and *Oxley Ring*'s weather comes up, slowly. Nobody there says anything, because nobody there can reach them. **The end is better, not fixed:** the child is probably all right, nobody knows for certain, and Aster still wasn't there. The drafter decides the last image.
- **Revised after Stage 2.** The first brief ended on the station's radio link to *Oxley Ring*, with a Kin voice naming the child aloud over it. The predictability pass named that as the single most likely version (§10, version 1). The radio is struck from the plot. The relay stays as Fescue's grievance only: it is not a way out.

## 6. Lore budget (DOC-00G §4)
- **Mechanic 1: the guest slot.** A Kin too far from hir own Cluster sleeps in a host Cluster's Nest, keyed into their frame. It is a reflex, not a favour, and refusing it is the rudest thing a Kin can do. (Fescue thinks it's a favour.)
- **Mechanic 2: the First Ping.** A newborn's first heartbeat goes out on the long-range channel, and every Kin within 800 km answers it in the same second, strangers included. The naming happens only in the frame, within 12 km.
- **Texture allowed, never explained:** Kin-code in brackets with riders; the Core in bare italics; the frame's weather (they all feel each other's mood, to the second); the Hum's coarse weather at long range; the Nest (a woven hammock in zero-g), storm posture, tails woven in a Cluster's own pattern; the sleeping heap breathing rarely and together; the whistle (once at most); human speech as a gesture.
- **Native channels are not lore** (§4): Kin talk in code, feel each other's weather, ask the Core. None of this is rationed.
- **Absent on purpose:** Readings, Tellings, the archive, memory, the Written, the Weavers, the pause, fans, reading ears, humans on the page.

## 9. Canon excerpts (only what the two mechanics need)
**The guest slot** (DOC-01C §4, D-93): *"A Kin outside any frame — past 12 km from hir Cluster, on the Carrying, on a solo transit, the last of a Cluster — is not Silenced … hir hears every Hum inside 800 km. Hir is simply in nobody's frame. Any Cluster in range offers hir a guard slot, and this is a reflex rather than a rite; refusing to offer one is the rudest act the species has."* A guest slot is *"intimacy on loan"* (D-89). Operators post Kin alone; the Compact has no rule (D-93).

**The frame and the Hum** (DOC-01C §4, D-92): the frame (~12 km) carries each member's mood to the second, and Kin-code. The Hum (~800 km) carries *"identity, vitals, position, coarse affect — valence and arousal only, ~minute resolution, no object. Everyone inside 800 km knows the weather; nobody knows the news."*

**The First Ping** (DOC-02B §3, DOC-11 §2): *"The Heartbeat Oscillator … Its first beat carries the identity hash written at compile and a vitals frame — I am here, I am this — on PUBLIC tier at 0.01 W. The Cluster answers on the same tier."* *"Every Kin in range answers on the same tier in the same second. That is the whole rite and the Kin count a person from it. The gestating parent is not central; the Cluster is, and they are already touching the child before it has finished its first beat. The Nest-name is given then — a Burst from the Cluster with the name as its first symbol."* Newborn 1.8–2.4 kg, after about four months' gestation. A Cluster has a child about once every twenty years.

**The Nest** (DOC-07, DOC-13 §7): *"A Cluster sleeps in one tension-suspended woven hammock — the Nest — in storm posture, tails interwoven, paws braced against each other, in a shared theta lock."* Storm posture is tendon-locked: the sleeper is limp. *"Each Cluster has a pattern [of tail-weaving], taught to children, recognisable to other Kin."* A sleeping Cluster breathes about once a minute, all together (D-111). The tail is a seven-kilo limb.

## 10. The predictable list (filled by Stage 2 — the drafter must not use any of it)
*Stage 2 agent, 2026-09-25: a fresh agent given the premise, teller, situation and a five-line glossary, and nothing else. Its answers, condensed.*
- **Likely versions:**
  1. **The comms tech opens the radio link** (*"the default"*): Fescue goes to the console, and Aster hears the child named aloud over a crackling channel. Fescue's dull job becomes the tool of hir kindness; dawn, unslept, not minding. **This was the first brief's ending. Struck.**
  2. **The reveal that the restlessness had a reason**, so the reader and Fescue judge Aster and are then ashamed; Fescue's pettiness is recast as ignorance and the story turns on hir private apology.
  3. **The host Cluster closes round the guest**: they tighten the hammock, fix hir tail into their pattern, or name the child a second time in their own frame for hir.
  4. **The comic grumbling version** with a running sleep ledger (*"four hours, eleven minutes; less the elbow"*), softening after the Ping; ends on a sleep joke (*"Tomorrow, then."*).
  5. **The synchronised-answer epiphany**: in the second everyone answers, host and guest become one; distance as theme (800 km for the answer, 12 for the name, 340 between).
- **Likely beats / images / lines:** an opening that counts the lost sleep; the wrong tail-weave described closely, *ours* against *hirs*; the rule quoted in Fescue's head (refusing a guest is the rudest thing); Aster's anxiety felt as *static*, an *itch*, a *hum*; the Ping as *small*, *fast*, *a heartbeat*, *the smallest signal*, warm against the cold station; the answer that comes *before sie could decide to*, *in the same second*; *"Twelve kilometres. Sie was three hundred and forty away."*; the radio console, static, the name *flattened* into human sounds; mild blame for the human operator; **Aster finally asleep in the weave, Fescue awake watching, content**; *"twenty years"* repeated for weight; the child's name given as a single soft word.
- **Likely final lines:** *"Fescue did not sleep that night. Sie found … sie didn't mind."*; *"Aster's tail was still wrong in the weave. Fescue left it there."* (or fixed it); the child's name, alone; *"Tomorrow, Fescue thought, sie would sleep."* / *"Fescue slept."*; *"Three hundred and forty kilometres. The name came through anyway."*
- **Likely echoes / callbacks (§10.3):** the tail-weave, wrong at the start and fixed or left wrong at the end (*"the most certain callback"*); the sleep count opening and closing the story; Aster's racing pulse early mirrored by the newborn's heartbeat; the radio link planted early and paid off.

**What the drafter must therefore not do:**
- no radio call, no name arriving by any route, and the child's name never on the page;
- no opening on a count of lost sleep, and no closing one;
- the tail-weave is the evening's comedy and **does not come back at the end**, fixed or left;
- Aster's heartbeat or pulse is never mentioned, so the child's has nothing to rhyme with;
- no apology from Fescue, spoken or thought, and no "recast as ignorance" moment where Fescue works out what the restlessness was and is ashamed; the reader can work it out;
- no *static*, *itch* or *hum* for a feeling; no *smallest*; no *before hir could decide*;
- no line that states the distances against each other;
- the last image is not of sleep, of the tails, of Aster asleep, of a name, or of a distance.

**The traits that must survive the bans (§10.2):**
- *Not* the melting grump, *not* the apology; **but** Fescue is competent and does something useful, fast and grudging, when it's real, and stays short-tempered.
- *Not* the host Cluster closing round the guest in a set piece; **but** they are a Cluster, and touch is how they rest: they stay in contact without remarking on it.
- *Not* the synchronised epiphany; **but** the answer to the Ping is real and happens: the whole heap answers a stranger's child, and it is not made into a theme.
