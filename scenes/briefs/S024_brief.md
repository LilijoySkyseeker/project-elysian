# BRIEF — S024 *Outer Relay* (working title)
**Slate entry:** none. A new premise, chosen under the owner's full creative direction (2026-09-24). **Ledger gap it fills:** a Kin who is wrong and loses to a human who deserved to win (DOC-00B §10; never yet written), a human near the viewpoint who is sharp and petty and stays that way, an ending cut mid-action, and the collection's second present-tense scene. The slate's *"cheap check, mid-argument, unremarked"* rides along.
**Status:** brief → predicted → drafted → linted → cold-read → revised → **owner** → canon → filed

---

## 1. The want
Pell wants six months somewhere nobody can tell how embarrassed hir is.

## 2. The obstacle / what goes wrong after the middle
- **Obstacle:** the one person who can sign the posting, Agnes Ruhl, won't. Her reason is plain and a little selfish: the ring refit starts on the ninth and Pell is her best snub hand. She does not care that Pell is embarrassed, and says so.
- **After the middle:** Pell decides the refit is a cover and that Agnes is really refusing because of the one Kin she ever posted past 800 km (Vetch-2, nineteen years ago, came back thin). Pell produces it exactly: date, form, Agnes's signature, what she said at the time. **Agnes does not remember who Vetch-2 was.** She is not hiding anything. It was nineteen years and hundreds of forms ago. The Kin's perfect weapon lands on nothing; Pell was wrong about her. Then Pell, humiliated a second time in a week, reaches for something that *will* land: what Agnes said to hir, privately, one night on a ring shift eleven months after her husband left. Word for word, with the date. It lands. It cannot be taken back, because Pell said it exactly and both of them know Pell could say it again.

## 3. What is lost
Agnes's regard. She has been the one human on the station who talks to Pell like a colleague and not a mascot. At the end she calls hir *Pell-3*, formally, for the first time in thirty years, and asks hir to shut the door on the way out. There is no apology scene.

## 4. Voice card (DOC-00G §5.1)

| Who | Pell-3, a Kin of about ninety, rigger on the ring crew of *Halcyon-3*. Competent, quick, proud of being quick. Four days ago hir gave a toast at a human wedding in the ring mess, in human speech, and it went badly. |
| To whom, why now | Nobody. Close third, **present tense**, pinned to Pell for one lunch hour in a postings office. |

| Syntax | Fast, argumentative, lawyerly: numbered points, "firstly", sub-clauses that anticipate objections. **The mind shows in the syntax:** when Pell reaches for a fact, the exact thing arrives mid-sentence, dated and verbatim, and the sentence carries on without comment. That is how the cheap check looks from inside (D-103): it costs nothing and is never remarked on. The shame shows as interruptions, where the toast surfaces unbidden and the sentence swerves. |

## 5. Shape
- **Form:** short story, single scene, continuous time (about 40 minutes).
- **Tense & person:** present, close third.
- **Target length:** about 2,200 words.
- **Ending type:** **cut mid-action.** Pell leaves the office for the ring, where the refit rigging is already going up, and gets on with work: clipping on, taking a line. The scene stops mid-task, on something physical and ordinary, with Agnes's last word still being true. **Not** on the Cluster's Kin-code, the weather clearing, stars, or the relay (all on the predictable list). Whether the wedding went badly is never resolved either way.

## 6. Lore budget (DOC-00G §4)
- **Mechanic 1 — the weather is public.** Everyone within about 800 km feels a Kin's mood as valence and arousal, no object. Hir own Cluster, close by, feels it finely and answers. So every time the toast surfaces in Pell, hir Cluster feels the spike and sends warmth, and the warmth reminds hir, and it spikes again. **The loop is the comedy and the misery.** Only an outer relay, past 800 km, has "nothing in the sky".
- **Mechanic 2 — the cheap check.** Facts are free and exact: naming a date or a thing fetches it verbatim in about a second. Nobody can bluff about a fact in front of a Kin. What costs is going back to *feel* something (the gloss ⟨don't make me read it⟩). Pell will not replay the toast itself. Hir can quote everything else.
- **Texture, allowed, never explained:** Kin-code as *[words ~ rider]*; the Cluster by name; the frame; the posting form; the relay's name; Agnes's console showing Pell's weather as a line; the 12 km and 800 km distances as numbers on a form, never explained.
- **Absent on purpose:** the pause, the Core, the ears-as-heat misread, the fan, a tail round an ankle, a hand on a chest, the number-two pump, seventeen minutes, any rite. **No "eleven" and no "four" as a count.**
- **What the posting costs, which Agnes knows and Pell knows and neither explains:** months beyond 800 km alone *write thin*. A Kin comes home able to recite the time and unable to feel it, poorer, in the only currency they have. Agnes once signed one: Vetch-2, nineteen years ago. Pell holds it exactly. Agnes has forgotten it entirely. **Neither guilt nor a confession is in this scene** (both are on the predictable list).

## 7. Three details that demonstrate nothing (§2.3)
1. Agnes eats sunflower seeds from a paper twist and spits the shells into her own cupped hand, then tips the hand into the bin at intervals.
2. Throughout the meeting she is re-crimping a zip pull on a work jacket with a pair of needle-nose pliers, and does not finish.
3. The posting form is revision F. Pell's copy is revision E. Agnes points this out, and it matters to nobody.
4. A sticky note on the edge of her console reads *BERNARD — NO*. It is never explained.

## 10. The predictable list (filled by Stage 2; the drafter must not use any of it)
A fresh agent, given only the premise, predicted:
- **Likely versions:** (1) Agnes refuses kindly and confesses her own public humiliation, and Pell feels less alone; (2) it turns out the couple loved the toast, and the embarrassment was only in Pell's head; (3) Agnes refuses out of guilt over a Kin she once posted who went wrong, and Pell ends up comforting *her*; (4) the climax is Pell asking the Cluster directly in Kin-code, *[Please stop…]*, and they answer *[We're staying]*; (5) a compromise posting, the silence is unbearable, and Pell comes home glad to be felt.
- **Likely beats:** the toast told in flashback; the Cluster's sympathy as literal weather (a warm front, humid with pity); Pell rehearsing and fumbling the request; the dead plant, the joke mug, the paper form; Agnes saying no before Pell finishes, or turning the form face-down; Pell envying Agnes because nobody can feel her; *"You can't outrun your own feelings, not even at 800 km"*; humans wishing to be felt, so the curse becomes a gift; the Cluster's small domestic Kin-code at the end; the relay as stars, silence and one blinking light.
- **Likely final lines:** the Cluster felt the weather clear; *[We're still here]* and Pell let them; hir weather was hir own for once; Agnes plays the toast recording, quietly; 800 km was never far enough and never needed to be.
- **Brief changes made in response:** Agnes has no confession and no guilt, and does not remember the Kin she posted; the details were replaced; the ending moved off the Cluster, the weather and the reframed toast. The toast is **never told in flashback**: Pell will not replay it (⟨don't make me read it⟩), so the reader gets only fragments that surface against hir will.
