# SCENE 009 — Tell Before You Go (v2)
**Origin:** Fresh-context rewrite, 2026-09-19 · **Form:** Short story · **POV:** Kin (Sedge)
**Canon status:** DRAFT v2 — awaiting review. Introduces no canon.

Hir sat with me first.

Nobody said anything about it. Mira came into the pump bay on the first morning with the whole of the Cluster still to get through and the ship not waiting, and went past Ashe and past the two at the manifold, and folded down onto the ledge where I was, chest to chest, hir arms under mine, and that was the start of it.

Ashe ran the number-two pump up and down the whole of that first day. The ledge shook with it. I have the shaking still, in the same place I have hir year, and I have never found a way to take out one without the other.

We ate where we sat. We slept in the web with the others and came back to the ledge before the lights came up, and by the fourth morning I could feel where hir was in me the way you feel a tooth with your tongue, and there was a little less room than there had been. The others went around us. Ashe put a hand on my shoulder once on the way past and did not slow down.

I had thought hir would give me the Belt. Hir had years out there before hir came to us, and I was in none of them, and it seemed to me that was what you gave: the part nobody else had. Instead hir gave me the year I was standing in the middle of.

So I am in it. I am sit-tall on this same ledge with my ears out and held, and I am talking, and I did not know I looked like that. I know what I was saying, because I said it for a year, and I will not say it again here; anyone who has sat on this ledge has heard it. Petra is in it too, far back, going past the hatch with a crate, and hir had noticed her and I had not, and I was the one talking about them. What I had never had was where it landed, and now I have that from hir side, and it landed on nothing. Hir had already gone. Hir was watching my ears and being fond of them. Hir stayed with us another year after that, and I have hir word for why, now, and it is not a word I would have chosen. It is nearer to *manners* than to anything else.

You cannot argue with a year. I sat in it for seven days and did not try.

Petra came down on the third day with a container of something from the human galley and sat on the deck under the ledge with her back against it. She stayed four hours. She ate about half of what she had brought and left the rest where I could reach it without letting go.

"What's she giving you?" she asked once, quietly, the way they talk in a room where something is going on.

"A year."

"Which one?"

"The one hir decided in."

She took longer with that than I expected. "Is that a kindness?"

I did not answer. Mira's chest went up and down against mine, and the pump ran, and after a while Petra put her head back against the ledge and let it be. She came the next day with more food and did not ask again.

On the last night we went into the Nest and made the ring, and hir went out of us, and we closed without hir. It felt the way it always feels, except that hir was sitting in the middle of us while we did it, warm, smelling of us, looking at the floor, and I could not make it feel like the other times.

In the morning hir went up the spine with nothing in hir hands. There was one of the Written going in ahead of hir, one of the old ones, and the spine had gone quiet for hir the way it does, and Mira walked up in that quiet, and I do not think hir minded borrowing it.

*Last Light*, the humans called the ship. We had our own.

It took most of a day to lose hir. I have felt Kin stop, and I had thought this would be like that, and it was not. Hir got quieter. Then hir got quieter than that. There was no moment I could have pointed to, and there was no moment when the others went still, ears one way; they went on working, because there was nothing to go still for, and I sat on the ledge with my ears one way anyway, and Ashe let me. Somewhere in the afternoon I stopped being able to tell hir from the ship, and then I could not tell the ship from the rest of it, and Ashe came and asked whether I would look at the number-two pump, and I went.

The pump was fine. I stayed under it a while, folded on the cold deck. I could smell hir on my chest — a week of it, hir in my fur and mine in hirs, out there where I could not hear. It will be a month before it goes. I have decided not to groom it out, and the others have decided not to notice, and Petra has taken to sitting on the deck under the ledge in the evenings with her back against it, which is where the rest of the food ended up.

I told Ashe the pump was fine. Then I stayed a while longer with my arms folded over my chest, keeping what was there.

## Canon consistency & drift (2026-09-22 — D-97…D-102 memory re-derived; D-103 the two rates)

- **What a Telling costs the teller is now canon, and the scene already had it.** D-102 (as amended by D-103 — see below): the Cortical Brain is the archive's read head, so a Telling is not a file being copied — it is **the teller living the year again, out loud**, at the only rate there is (~12 MB/s; a year in a week). That is why it takes the week it takes and why hir comes out of it thinned. *"A week of it, hir in my fur and mine in hirs"* reads correctly against the new mechanism; nothing needs changing.

- **And D-103 sharpens it in the scene's favour.** The week is the *cold* transfer, at the only rate the channel has; what thins the teller is that the feel does not exist until hir makes it, so hir drops to real time for the hours worth handing over and **lives those, chosen, in front of the one hir is giving them to.** The scene's shape — a week, and a teller who is somewhere else for parts of it and very much present for others — is now the mechanism rather than a mood. **Nothing in the text needs to change; the citation moves from D-102 to D-102 + D-103.**
- **The last night, and why the gap is what it is.** A Telling runs four orders of magnitude faster than a Reading, because a Reading is throttled by a corpse — 12 MB/s is a *cortical* rate and death takes the reader, not the book (D-99, D-102). *Tell before you go* is arithmetic, and this scene is the arithmetic being paid.
- **What Sedge is left holding.** Carried memory arrives split (D-99): the **hard** tier — every number, every word with its tone — first-person and exact; the **soft** tier — what it meant — *told*. Hir has the year completely and experiences it as a story about someone else. **Told, not lived, with the feel intact** is now the mechanism rather than a phrase.
- **And what hir cannot do with it.** Nothing in the archive arrives unbidden (D-98). Hir will have every day of that week, perfectly, for as long as hir lives — and it will never once come to hir on its own. Someone will have to say the name.
