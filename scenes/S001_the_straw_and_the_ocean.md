# SCENE 001 — The Straw and the Ocean
**Origin:** DOC-05 Scene 03 (v1.2) · **Theme:** The Bandwidth Gap · **POV:** Human (Cmdr Vance) / Kin (Kirin)
**Style:** DOC-00 governs names and pronouns (human POV says *she*; Kin POV and documentary voice say *hir*).  
**Canon status:** ORIGINAL TEXT PRESERVED — drift notes below; not yet revised.

---

Commander Vance leaned against the console, rubbing his eyes. "Tell me again, Kirin. Why did the coolant bypass trip?"

Kirin sat on the console ledge, hir taurform lower body resting comfortably on four folded limbs, hir two upper hands resting on hir chest. Hir ears swiveled toward Vance.

Kirin had already exchanged 400 megabytes of telemetry with the station computer, diagnosed a faulty relay, simulated three patch solutions, and updated the Cluster's duty logs—all in the last three seconds over hir bio-RF link.

But looking at the tired human before hir, Kirin intentionally slowed hir thoughts down to a crawling trickle.

"The pressure valve on Loop B degraded," Kirin said slowly, vocalizing at a human-friendly 12 words per second. "I rerouted the fluid to Loop C. System is nominal."

Vance blinked. "Just like that? You didn't even touch the manual bypass wheel."

"I didn't need to," Kirin smiled, hir tail flicking softly. Kirin felt the familiar, gentle pang of the Pet/Protector complex—a quiet fond patience for this slow, fragile organic creator who processed reality through a straw while Kirin swam in an ocean of light. "Go sleep, Vance. We have the watch."

## Canon drift since written (2026-09-19)
- **D-12 ceiling:** Kirin did not "diagnose" the relay or "simulate three patch solutions" — the *station computer* did; Kirin pulled 400 MB of telemetry over the mesh and read it in three seconds. The scene reads correctly if "diagnosed" becomes "read the diagnosis." Kirin then *chose* between the computer's three patches — that part is hirs.
- **D-19 documentation flaw:** Kirin's spoken report to Vance is a *courtesy translation* — the same gesture as slowing to 12 words/s. If Kirin later files a written log it will be terse and one line will leak affect ("*Vance was tired; discuss overtime.*").
- **Bandwidth Gap runs both ways (DOC-01C §8):** Kirin's "fond patience" can carry a second layer — to Kirin, Vance is content with almost nothing.
- **DOC-01A §6:** "resting comfortably on four folded limbs, upper hands on chest" on a console ledge = the *sit-tall* posture. Kirin's tail flick is canon-consistent; hir ears swivelling toward Vance is the mechanical steer of the arrays.
- **"Just like that? You didn't even touch the manual bypass wheel."** Still right — physical redundancy (DOC-03 §2) exists for humans and overrides; Kirin used the native interface.
- **D-68/D-69 (2026-09-20):** the sit-tall posture and "two upper hands resting on hir chest" survive exactly. Note for any revision: the four folded limbs end in *hand-paws*, not feet — five digits, opposable thumbs. Kirin's tail flick is now a seven-kilo limb moving, not a flick of floof; "softly" still works, but it is a heavier gesture than it reads.
