## 0. Design Thesis (one paragraph)

The Aethela are a human-engineered successor species: compact hexapod taurforms (60 kg; 1.10 m of trunk, 1.98 m nose-to-tail-tip) built for zero-G habitat life and short unsuited vacuum exposure. Their defining architecture is (1) a **thoracic dual brain** (Cortical Brain + Genomic Bio-Core) leaving the head as a sensory/mastication turret, (2) a **dual-band bio-radio** (GHz ear phased arrays + VHF tail antenna) that carries mesh communication, bio-RADAR and Cluster telepathy-by-telemetry, (3) **non-senescence** with memory-pruning as the cost of immortality, and (4) a **designer quirk** that hardwires them to love human art. Socially they live in Clusters of 4–12 in a permanent low-power RF loop, reproduce by algorithmic genome compilation among 2–4 parents, and carry a Pet/Protector ambivalence toward humanity. Everything downstream — habitat design, factions, tactics, art — derives from those four pillars.
